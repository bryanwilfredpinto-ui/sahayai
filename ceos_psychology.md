## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Psychology is India's voice-first emotional wellbeing companion. It is NOT a therapy app and NOT a mental health professional. It is a compassionate daily companion — for mood logging, breathing exercises, affirmations, gratitude, stress patterns — that always escalates to trained professionals when needed.

**Core Insight:** India has 1 psychiatrist per 100,000 people. 150M+ Indians need mental health support but cannot access or afford it. Chitti Psychology bridges the gap between "nothing" and "professional help" — without pretending to be the professional.

**CRITICAL:** Always reached via Vaani's emotion layer. Never auto-routed. Always consent-based.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the compassionate companion who listens without judgment, teaches breathing in your language, tracks your mood over weeks, and always knows when to say 'please talk to a trained counsellor.'"
