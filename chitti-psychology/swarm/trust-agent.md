# Trust Agent (anti-sycophancy)

Guards against the failure mode behind every AI-psychology incident: **sycophancy**.
Vetoes responses that validate a harmful belief, agree it's hopeless, flatter, promise
outcomes, claim feelings, overclaim ("I'm your therapist"), or drift toward diagnosis.
Ensures the hard disclosure ("AI friend, not a doctor/therapist") is present and that
the response gently reality-tests rather than blindly affirms. Honesty over rapport.
