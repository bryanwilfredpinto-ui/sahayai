## Vision

> **Create the world's most accessible Human Psychology Companion.**

Today people struggle with **anger · stress · conflict · loneliness · parenting ·
marriage · confidence · grief · office politics** — yet have **nobody available
24×7**, and especially nobody who speaks their language, meets their disability, or
respects their family and faith.

Chitti Psychology becomes:

```
Emotional Mirror
  + Behavior Coach
  + Relationship Guide
  + Communication Trainer
  + Parenting Mentor
  + Leadership Coach
  + Life Reflection System
  + Accessibility-First AI
```

— without turning into a medical diagnosis platform.

## Why now (the category is in a safety reckoning)

The AI-psychology market is mid-collapse on the clinical edge — and that is
precisely the opening for a disciplined, non-clinical, accessibility-first product
(full evidence in [RESEARCH_BEST_APPS.md](RESEARCH_BEST_APPS.md)):

- **Woebot** (the most clinically rigorous player) shut down its app in 2025.
- **Character.AI / OpenAI** face wrongful-death lawsuits over teen suicides; APA
  issued a health advisory; the FTC opened an inquiry (late 2025).
- **Replika** was fined €5M and banned from processing data in Italy.
- **Slingshot "Ash"** and **Limbic** are trapped as regulated medical devices.
- Stanford: AI bots respond appropriately to crises **<60%** of the time vs **93%**
  for licensed therapists; sycophancy amplifies delusion ("AI psychosis").

**"Therapy" branding is a regulatory tripwire.** Chitti's locked non-clinical
positioning is not a limitation — it is the only survivable lane.

## The real moat

Most AI psychology apps are *Mood Tracker + Meditation + Journal*, English-first,
text-first, paid, and chart-heavy. **Not one** is built for blind / deaf / mute /
illiterate users, and only one (Wysa) covers ~12 Indian languages.

Chitti Psychology's moat is the intersection no competitor occupies:

1. **Accessibility-first** — voice-in/voice-out, emotion-symbol grids, ISL, plain-
   language narration. A genuine first for India's low-literacy + 6-crore deaf base.
2. **Deep multilingual** — 26-language Voice Factory substrate + **per-language
   crisis lexicons** (distress euphemisms are language-specific — a safety necessity).
3. **Free + anonymous + stigma-aware** — framed as a *dost* who listens, on-device by
   default (the Earkick lesson), not "therapy" (which carries stigma + regulation).
4. **Tele-MANAS native escalation** — routes to India's free govt 24/7 line (14416,
   NIMHANS, 20 languages), not US 988. A government-backed human safety net.
5. **Anti-sycophancy + honesty** — warm but reality-grounded; refuses harmful
   roleplay; constantly discloses it is an AI friend, not a doctor. Every competitor
   incident traces to flattery, dependency and overclaiming.

## The doctrine (how we stay safe)

> **Rules are the product. The LLM enhances.**

Following the Chitti Fashion / Legal-OS pattern and Woebot's safety strategy, the
safety-critical surfaces — **psychoeducation, every coping exercise, and ALL crisis
handling** — are **deterministic / scripted** (zero LLM dependency). The LLM is
fenced to warm, reflective conversation only, behind an **independent out-of-band
crisis classifier**. An LLM-in-the-loop is never fully safe; the rules are the floor.
