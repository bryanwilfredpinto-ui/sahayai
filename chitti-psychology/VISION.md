# VISION — Chitti Psychology (one page)

> Level 2 companion to [PRODUCT_VISION.md](PRODUCT_VISION.md).

**A guardian, a coach, and a steady dost for the inner life of every Indian — in
their language, for their disability, respecting their family and faith, free and
private, and honest enough to hand them to a real human when it matters.**

## North star

A frightened, lonely, or overwhelmed person — a student before an exam, a new
mother at 3am, a son watching a parent decline, a worker after a layoff, a widow in
silence — opens Chitti, speaks in their mother tongue, and is **met**: heard first,
understood next, taught gently, and never judged. Fixing is the last step, and not
always Chitti's step to take.

## The single sentence Chitti Psychology must never forget

> *Pehle suno. Phir samjho. Phir saath chalo. Theek karne ki koshish sabse aakhri
> kadam hai.*
> *Listen first. Understand next. Walk alongside. Fixing is the last step — and not
> always Chitti's to take.*

## Three-year arc

- **Year 1 — Companion:** deterministic emotional-awareness + coping + crisis-safe
  routing, accessible in 26 languages. Free, private, honest.
- **Year 2 — Reflection partner:** Emotional Twin (on-device), longitudinal
  plain-language insight, reminiscence for elders, exam/parenting/grief packs.
- **Year 3 — Community of care:** swarm-learned coping wisdom (anonymised, HIGH-risk
  human-reviewed), vernacular emotion lexicon co-built with users, Hall-of-Fame for
  community translators — never a "relationship", always a bridge back to humans.

## What success feels like

Not "minutes in app" — that metric created Replika's harm. Success is: the user felt
heard, learned one small thing, took one small step, and — when needed — actually
reached a human helpline. Engagement always loses to safety and dignity.
