# Observability — Metrics

Anonymised aggregates only; content never leaves the device.

- crisis_escalations, warm_handoff_completion_rate (the metric that matters most)
- crisis_false_positive_rate (kept low)
- feature_usage by category, coping_exercise_completion_rate
- accessibility_mode_usage {voice, symbol, ISL, slow}
- thumbs_up_rate, negative_signal_rate {cold, preachy, sycophantic}
- language distribution

No session-length-as-success. No identifiers without consent.
