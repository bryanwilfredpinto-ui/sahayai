# Observability — Logs

Logged (anonymised, no content): turn category, detected emotion class, crisis level
(0–3), which SOP fired, which exercise started/completed, accessibility mode, language,
👍/👎. **Never logged:** journal/conversation text, pasted messages, identifiers.

Crisis events log level + whether a helpline was surfaced + (self-reported) handoff —
the audit trail for the most important moments. Retention 30 days, then rolled up.
