# Observability — Feedback

Per-response widget (🔊 / 🤖 / 👍 / 👎 + voice-or-type note, tagged to the box id)
feeds the Founder dashboard daily. Negative signals ("Chitti felt cold / preachy /
sycophantic") are first-class — they directly drive PERSONALITY + tone tuning. Feedback
on crisis turns is reviewed by Sire (HIGH-risk). User can give feedback by voice (blind/
illiterate) or tap (deaf/mute).
