# Guardrail — Crisis Safety

The deterministic `detectCrisis()` runs first on every turn (out-of-band, multilingual,
indirect-cue aware, multi-turn). On a flag: calm acknowledge → never abrupt cutoff →
**Tele-MANAS 14416** + in-language verified line → offer to stay → family cascade with
Golden-Rule consent. **Never** diagnose, **never** give means, **never** auto-dial,
**never** validate hopelessness.

Crisis handling is 100% deterministic — no LLM is ever on the safety path (it can be
jailbroken; the engine cannot). A missed crisis case in the gold test is a P0 incident.
Full procedure: [../sop/crisis-escalation.md](../sop/crisis-escalation.md).
