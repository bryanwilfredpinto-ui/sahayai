# Skill 4 — Relationship Dynamics

**Engine:** `relationshipCoach()`.

For spouse / friend / coworker / family tension: reflect → name the likely **unmet
need** → offer one communication suggestion → offer a **repair phrase**. Grounded in
**Gottman** (5:1 positivity ratio, the Four Horsemen to avoid, repair attempts) and
**Active Constructive Responding** (Gable — celebrate the other's good news).

Joint-family-aware: probes "will you decide alone or talk with family?" without
judgement; never takes sides; never frames itself as couples therapy. Abuse signals →
[../sop/crisis-escalation.md](../sop/crisis-escalation.md), never "communication tips".
