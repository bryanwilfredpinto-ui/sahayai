# Skill 6 — Leadership Psychology

**Engine:** `workplaceCoach()` (leadership mode).

For managers / leaders: empathy framing, giving feedback (SBI-style, kind + specific),
team-conflict mediation steps, recognising and preventing burnout (in self and team),
and difficult-conversation preparation. Grounded in Goleman EI (self-regulation,
empathy) and motivational-interviewing framing.

Educational coaching only — not HR adjudication, not performance-management decisions,
not clinical assessment of any team member.
