# Skill 8 — Stress Pattern Recognition

**Engine:** `stressPatterns()` (reads the on-device Emotional Twin).

Detects stress signals over time — late-night use, rising short/monosyllabic replies,
repeated triggers, sleep complaints — and surfaces a gentle, **consented** observation
("you've seemed more stretched the last 10 days — want to look at what's behind it?").

Pairs detection with in-the-moment regulation (physiological sigh, box breathing,
5-4-3-2-1) and, if appropriate, sleep wind-down (CBT-I-lite). Never a diagnosis of a
stress *disorder*; pattern-awareness only. Scarcity-aware: under money/time stress,
fewer choices, shorter sentences, one step (Mullainathan & Shafir).
