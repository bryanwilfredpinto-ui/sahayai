# Skill 7 — Habit Formation

**Engine:** `ifThen()` + `woop()` + `behavioralActivation()`.

Turns a goal into action using evidence-based, deterministic tools:
- **Implementation intentions** — "If situation X, then I will do Y" (Gollwitzer).
- **WOOP / MCII** — Wish · Outcome · Obstacle · Plan (Oettingen).
- **Behavioral activation** — schedule small, value-aligned activities to break the
  low-mood → inactivity spiral (framed as a coping habit, never depression treatment).

Successive approximation (Skinner): one tiny step, success, genuine praise, next step.
Gentle, never punitive, never streak-shaming.
