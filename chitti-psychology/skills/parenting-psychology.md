# Skill 5 — Parenting Psychology

**Engine:** `parentingGuide(age, behaviour)`.

Age-specific, developmentally grounded guidance (Erikson stages, Vygotsky ZPD) for
toddlers / children / teenagers. Example: "14-yr-old refusing to study" → *possible*
reasons (stress / burnout / lack of motivation) + a recommended, respectful
conversation opener.

**Never labels or diagnoses the child** (no "your child has ADHD/ODD"). Calm-first for
stressed parents (regulate the parent before advising). Postpartum-rage coping for new
parents (R16). Child-harm signals → crisis SOP + **Childline 1098**.
