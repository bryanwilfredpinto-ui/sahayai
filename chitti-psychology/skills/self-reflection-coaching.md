# Skill 10 — Self Reflection Coaching

**Engine:** `journalPrompt()` + `threeGoodThings()` + `valuesSort()` + `weeklyInsight()`.

Guided journaling: *what happened → how you felt → one small next step* (voice
journaling for non-readers). Hosts **Three Good Things** (nightly gratitude, Seligman/
GGIA), **ACT values card sort** (get unstuck), and **self-compassion** reframes (Neff)
for the inner critic.

Produces a weekly, **plain-language, narrated** insight ("you felt calmer on days you
talked to family") from the on-device Emotional Twin — never charts-only, never a
diagnosis, always opt-in and forgettable.
