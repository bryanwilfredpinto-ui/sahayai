# What can Chitti Psychology do for you?

> Read aloud by the 💡 Feature Discovery box (`chitti_features.js`). Plain language,
> voice-first. Every line is a thing you can ask for by voice or tap.

- **"I feel..."** — I'll gently name what you might be feeling and listen. 🪞
- **"Calm me down"** — a 30-second breathing or grounding exercise, spoken with you. 🌬️
- **"I'm about to lose it"** — an SOS to ride the anger/urge until it passes. 🌊
- **"Help me understand this feeling"** — a simple explainer, in your language. 📖
- **"What can I do when I feel anxious / sad / angry?"** — small steps that help. 🧰
- **"Say it without a fight"** — I'll help you word a hard message kindly. 💬
- **"My partner/friend/family and I are fighting"** — relationship coaching. 💞
- **"My child won't listen / my teen won't study"** — parenting help by age. 👨‍👩‍👧
- **"Work is stressing me out"** — burnout, feedback, difficult conversations. 💼
- **"I lost someone / my job"** — grief companion (I sit with you, I don't fix). 🕯️
- **"Help me feel confident"** — for interviews, presentations, public speaking. ✨
- **"Help me sleep"** — a wind-down routine for restless nights. 🌙
- **"I can't stop worrying about money"** — coping with money stress. 🧘
- **"I feel so alone"** — companionship + a gentle nudge to reach one person. 🤝
- **Journal** — speak or tap how your day went; I'll remember (only on your phone). 📓
- **Three good things** — end the day naming three good moments. 🌟

> I am Chitti, an AI friend who listens — **not a doctor, not a therapist.** If you
> are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, I will help you reach a real person
> right now: **Tele-MANAS 14416** (free, 24×7, your language). You are not alone.
