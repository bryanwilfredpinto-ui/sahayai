# SOP-002 — Workplace Conflict

**Trigger:** conflict / burnout / difficult feedback at work.

1. **Understand the issue** — what happened, who is involved.
2. **Separate facts from emotions** — reduce the heat before the plan.
3. **Recommend a response** — a kind, specific, blame-free message (SBI / NVC).
4. **Evaluate outcomes** — what might happen, what's in the user's control.

Add burnout check-in + urge-surfing for doom-scrolling if signalled. Educational
coaching only — never HR adjudication. End with per-response widget.
