# SOP-006 — Exam Stress

**Trigger:** student exam pressure (huge in India — ~81% report extreme exam stress).

1. **Calm first** — box breathing (R2) before anything else.
2. **Reframe** — catastrophic exam thoughts ("mera kuch nahi ho sakta") → counter-example
   from a past small win (Beck, gently).
3. **Plan realistically** — Pomodoro + a doable study window (not 12 hours).
4. **Protect sleep** — wind-down (R11); sleep is study, not weakness.
5. **Loop in parents** — gently, if helpful; never adds pressure.

Watch for crisis (exam-failure despair is a known risk) → crisis SOP. Per-response widget.
