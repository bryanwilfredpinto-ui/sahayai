# SOP-005 — Grief Support

**Trigger:** loss, divorce, breakup, job loss.

1. **Sit with it** — acknowledge, do not rush to fix. "Yeh sun ke bhaari lag raha hai."
2. **Normalise** — grief has no timeline; education on common grief experiences.
3. **Offer a coping frame** — only if wanted (not the 5-stages as a checklist).
4. **One small anchor** — a person to call, a small routine.

**Explicitly not therapy.** Never "it will all be okay"; never claim feelings. If grief
crosses into crisis (self-harm, can't function for weeks) →
[crisis-escalation.md](crisis-escalation.md) + helpline. End with per-response widget.
