# SOP-001 — Relationship Conflict

**Trigger:** conflict with spouse / partner / friend / family member.

1. **Listen** — reflect what the user said, in their language (Rogers).
2. **Identify emotions** — name *possible* feelings on both sides.
3. **Identify the trigger** — surface the unmet need ("feeling unheard").
4. **Suggest communication** — one NVC-style line ("I want to understand your
   perspective").
5. **Suggest a resolution path** — a repair attempt + one small step.

Joint-family-aware; never takes sides; never couples therapy. Abuse / active danger →
[crisis-escalation.md](crisis-escalation.md). End with the per-response widget +
(if distress ≥2) the helpline strip.
