# SOP-007 — Loneliness / Eldercare

**Trigger:** isolation, senior companionship, "I feel so alone".

1. **Be present** — a steady, unhurried check-in; slow voice for elders.
2. **Reminiscence** — guided "tell me about…" life-story prompts (improves mood,
   reduces loneliness; feeds the Emotional Twin).
3. **Reframe lonely thoughts** — CBT-informed, gentle ("no one cares" → a recent small
   connection).
4. **Nudge one connection** — help the user reach one person today (warm handoff to
   family / community), with Golden-Rule consent.

Erikson late-stage framing (integrity vs despair) — never rush an elder. Crisis cues →
crisis SOP. Per-response widget.
