# Accessibility — Elderly user (slow · large · simple)

- **Slower speech, lower pitch, simpler sentences, no jargon**, patient repetition.
- **Large text, high contrast, simple UI**, one task at a time.
- Loneliness is the dominant signal — daily check-in + **reminiscence / life-story**
  prompts (improve mood, reduce isolation; feed the Emotional Twin).
- Erikson late-stage framing (integrity vs despair) — give space to reminisce, never rush.
- Hearing/cognition aware: repeat patiently; "repetition is friendship, not failure".
