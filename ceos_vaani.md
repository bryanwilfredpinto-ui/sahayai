## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary

Chitti Vaani is India's most accessible, voice-first AI personal assistant — built as a native Android app that harnesses every hardware and software capability of the smartphone: microphone, camera, speaker, GPS, phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, email, YouTube, calendar, alarms, contacts, UPI payments and more.

**The Core Insight:** 900 million Indians are online, but millions cannot type — they are blind, deaf, mute, illiterate, elderly, or motor-impaired. Every other Chitti specialist (MedUPI, Legal, CA, Government, Health File, Fashion, 2-Wheeler, 4-Wheeler, Scanner, UPI Guard, News, Technical, Fundamentals, Psychology) is accessed THROUGH Vaani. The user never opens a specialist directly. Vaani is the one dost. Every capability. One conversation.

### 1.2 Vision Statement

"Be the digital equivalent of a brilliant, multilingual personal secretary who lives in every Indian's phone — available 24/7/365, speaks your language, handles your calls, reads your emails, plays your content, guides your navigation, pays your bills by voice, routes you to the right specialist instantly, and never judges your literacy level."

### 1.3 Mission

- Build the world's first fully accessible Indian voice OS for Android
- Be the SOLE user-facing surface — every Chitti specialist accessed through Vaani
- Support 26 Indian languages including tribal (Santali, Ho, Mundari, Oraon)
- Harness ALL Android phone capabilities via voice
- Serve 9 accessibility archetypes: blind, deaf, mute, illiterate, elderly, low-vision, cognitive, motor, rural
- Free, no sign-up, offline-capable for core features
- Community voices replace Bhashini over time — users donate voice, Hall of Fame rewards them

### 1.4 The Android Advantage

Chitti Vaani as Android app (not web) accesses:
Hotword detection ('Hey Chitti'), phone dialer, contacts, SMS, WhatsApp, Gmail, YouTube, calendar, alarms, GPS, UPI, camera/OCR, notifications, accessibility service, flashlight, Bluetooth, WiFi, biometrics, offline ASR — none of which are available to a web app.

## SECTION 7: VOICE COMMAND ENGINE

**5-Stage Pipeline:**
1. Wake Word — 'Hey Chitti' on-device detection (PocketSphinx / AI4Bharat hotword)
2. ASR — AI4Bharat IndicConformer → Bhashini cascade → Whisper fallback
3. Language ID — IndicBERT classifier
4. Intent + Entity — DeepSeek fast call (50 tokens max) or Gemini Nano on-device
5. Execute — Android Intent or Chitti skill router

**Emergency check runs BEFORE step 4 — no AI call needed for emergency.**
