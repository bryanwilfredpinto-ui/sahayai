# Chitti Jobs — Features

Your 24/7 AI career agent — fresher to CXO, any industry. Chitti knows you,
scores every job to your profile, drafts applications in your voice, and
tracks your pipeline. **You approve and send everything yourself.**

## Live now (BO1–BO10)

- **Knows you (memory-first)** — set up your profile once; Chitti remembers it on your device and greets you back. Never shared with other users.
- **Level-aware** — Chitti classifies you (fresher / junior / mid / senior / CXO) and adapts its tone and advice.
- **Score any job 1–10** — paste a job description and Chitti scores how well it fits your profile, with a transparent reason for every point.
- **ATS keyword check** — Chitti compares your resume to the job and shows your match %, plus the exact keywords you're missing. Applications are gated below 70% so you fix it first.
- **Auto-find from feeds** — Chitti can pull recent listings from Naukri & Indeed RSS for your target role (best-effort; paste is always available).
- **Drafts in your voice** — Chitti writes the application email and cover letter as you, level-appropriate, and handles career gaps honestly — never fabricating anything.
- **You send it** — Chitti opens your own email app with the draft ready (mailto). You review, edit, and send. Chitti never auto-sends.
- **Application pipeline** — every application tracked: applied → replied → interview → offer. Nothing falls through the cracks.

## How it works (your control, always)

- Chitti **never** sends an email, applies, or books anything without you. It drafts; you approve; you send.
- Chitti **never** invents experience, qualifications, or achievements.
- Free forever. No paywalls. No sign-up — you're a device, not an account.
