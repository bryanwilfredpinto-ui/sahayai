What this is

Search any Indian stock and see its fundamentals — Market Cap, P/E, P/B, EPS, dividend yield, 52-week range, debt-to-equity, latest quarterly revenue and net profit — each one explained in plain English with a Speak button.

Reference apps shamelessly copied

  • Angel One — Overview tab metrics (Market Cap, P/E, 52W H/L, dividend yield)
  • Zerodha Kite — Quarterly results table layout
  • Groww — Plain-English explanations on every metric card
  • Screener.in — Data depth and verdict-first chips

For three users

  • Blind: every card and verdict carries a 🔊 button. ARIA labels on every interactive element. Speech reads verdict first, reasoning second, numbers third.
  • Deaf / Mute: ▲▼ symbols and word labels (up, down, flat) on every change indicator. Colour is never the only signal.
  • Financially illiterate: every metric has a 6–12 word plain-English explanation. The verdict at the top says STRONG / MIXED / WEAK in three sentences max.
