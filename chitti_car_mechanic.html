❓ How to use Chitti Car Mechanic

Pick a button below. Diagnose a noise/warning light. Check if a mechanic’s quote is fair. Inspect a used car before buying. Get the right oil/tyre. See every renewal due (insurance, PUC, service). Every answer has a 🔊 to hear it aloud, a 🤖 to ask Chitti more, and 👍/👎/✏️ to tell us if it helped — in your language.

🗣️ Tell me what’s wrong

Describe the noise, smell or warning — e.g. “AC not cooling”, “grinding brakes”, “car won’t start”, “overheating”. Chitti tells you the likely cause, if it’s safe to drive, and whether you can fix it.

🔌 OBD warning code

Got a code from a scanner (like P0300)? Chitti explains it in plain words + tells you if it’s safe to drive.

🟢🟡🔴 Can I fix it myself?

Type a job — e.g. “wiper”, “bulb”, “brake pads”, “airbag”. Chitti tells you Safe-DIY 🟢, Caution 🟡, or Mechanic-only 🔴. Safety-critical work is always mechanic-only.

🎓 Understand my car (no jargon)

Short, plain explanations of every part — engine, brakes, tyres, battery, warning lights — and whether you can touch it safely.

🛡️ Is this mechanic’s quote fair?

Pick the job and enter the quoted price. Chitti compares it to the expected India range and flags overcharging.

🔍 Buying a used car? Check it first

Tick what’s true after inspecting. A failed critical check (accident, odometer, loan, engine) is a walk-away. Chitti also suggests a fair offer.

📜 Check the car's history before you pay

Enter the registration number. Chitti tells you exactly what to verify (accident, odometer, loan, challan) and opens the official portals. Chitti never invents a history.

🧮 EMI calculator

See your monthly EMI and the true total you'll pay, before you sign a car loan.

📋 Full 50-point inspection checklist

Walk the car with Chitti. Each point shows who can check it (you 🟢 or a mechanic 🔧) and whether it's critical (a fail = walk away).

💸 Selling your car?

Enter the market value. Chitti gives a listing price, a realistic selling price, and tips to get more.

🔔 What’s due on my car?

Chitti reads your saved car (the “My Car” tab) and lists everything due — insurance, PUC, service, timing belt — with how urgent it is. Save your dates in My Car first.

🛡️ Am I overpaying for insurance?

Enter your current premium. Chitti shows an indicative comparison ranked by claim-settlement ratio + price, so you can shop before renewing. (Always confirm the real quote.)

🌫️ PUC check

Enter your PUC expiry date. Chitti tells you if it’s valid and the fine risk if expired.

🛢️ Which engine oil should I use?

Chitti recommends the right grade for your fuel type. Always confirm against your owner’s manual.

🛞 Tyre check + recommendation

Enter tread depth (coin test) and/or the 4-digit DOT week/year on the sidewall. Chitti says if you must replace, and which tyre fits your use.

🔋 Battery check

Enter your battery’s install date. Chitti tells you when to get it tested or replaced.

⛽ Should I switch to CNG / EV?

Enter your current monthly fuel spend. Chitti computes the monthly saving and payback time — honestly, never a guarantee.

🧬 My Car — saved on this device only

Save your car once; Chitti remembers it for reminders & scores. Nothing leaves your phone. “Chitti forget” wipes it.

🩺 My car — health dashboard

One snapshot of every system (documents, service, battery, tyres, engine) from your saved car. Each shows a 🟢/🟡/🔴 with a word.

🪙 My savings this year

Chitti logs every rupee it helps you save (caught a scam, switched insurance…) toward the ₹10,000 goal.
