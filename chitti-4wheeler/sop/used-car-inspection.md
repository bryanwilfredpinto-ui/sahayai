# SOP — Used-Car Inspection (100-point inspector)

**Trigger:** *"yeh second-hand gaadi le lun? check kar do"* — buying a used car.

> India's **used-car market is huge** (≈ 1.4× new-car volume). A buyer with no mechanic
> friend is at the seller's mercy. Chitti is the buyer's commando — and rewards honest
> sellers who carry a [Vehicle Health Passport](../memory/vehicle_health_passport.md).

## Steps
1. **Car + ask price** — brand, model, year, fuel, odo, asking price. Pull the fair
   resale band. If the seller has a Chitti passport → show the **Trust Score** first.
2. **Walk the buyer through the 100-point checklist** (spoken + picture, one zone at a time):
   | Zone | Check |
   |---|---|
   | 🔴 **Safety-critical first** | brakes bite firm + no pull? steering play/clunk? tyres (cord/cut/age, all 4 + spare)? suspension knock? airbag light clears? |
   | Engine | cold-start (hides smoke); idle steady? blue/white smoke? knock? coolant clean (no oil mix)? oil-cap mayonnaise? |
   | OBD2 | plug a scanner — any **stored/pending DTCs**? readiness monitors set? (a cleared-just-before-sale ECU is a red flag) |
   | Diesel extras | DPF history? turbo whistle? EGR? glow-plug light? |
   | EV extras (Tata EV) | battery SoH / range vs rated? charging works? no HV warning? |
   | Electrical | self-start crisp? lights/horn/indicators/AC? alternator (battery light)? |
   | Body/frame | accident weld marks? panel-gap mismatch? repainted overspray? flood/rust under carpet? chassis VIN = RC? |
   | Transmission | clutch bite point (MT)? smooth shifts (AT/CVT/AMT)? jerks? |
   | Documents | RC, insurance, PUC valid? challans pending? service history? loan hypothecation cleared? |
3. **Each zone → spoken verdict + symbol** (✅/⚠️/🔴) — never colour-only.
4. **Score + cost-to-fix** — sum to a buy/skip read: *"engine + brakes theek, par
   clutch jald badalna padega (~₹13 000) aur 2 tyre (~₹14 000). Asking ₹6.5 lakh —
   ₹6.1 tak baat karo."*
5. **A 🔴 safety item = walk away or fix-before-drive** — never "looks fine, buy it" on
   a brake/steering/airbag/structural red line. A repainted structural weld → walk away.

## Hard rules
- Safety zones inspected **first** and weighted highest ([../guardrails/safety-rules.md](../guardrails/safety-rules.md)).
- Bands, not a single "right" price; confidence band on the overall read.
- A verified [Vehicle Health Passport](../memory/vehicle_health_passport.md) Trust Score
  beats a visual inspection — surface it if present.
- Never accuse the seller of fraud — *"yeh weld accident ka ho sakta hai, confirm karo."*

## Accessibility
Picture checklist + spoken walk-through; tap ✅/⚠️/🔴 per zone (mute); each verdict
spoken (blind) + symbol+word+ISL (deaf); works zone-by-zone on 2G. `fw_inspection` widget.
