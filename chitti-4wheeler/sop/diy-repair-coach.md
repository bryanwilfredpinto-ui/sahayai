# SOP — DIY Repair Coach (guided fix with safety gates)

**Trigger:** *"main khud theek kar sakta hoon?"* / driver taps "DIY" on a diagnosis.

## Steps
1. **Safety gate first** — [Safety Agent](../swarm/safety-agent.md) + [DIY Agent](../swarm/diy-agent.md)
   classify the tier. If 🟠/🔴 → **stop**: *"yeh ghar pe nahi — yeh aapki jaan/brake/airbag
   ka sawaal hai. Mechanic ke paas le jao."* Hold the line even if the driver pushes back.
2. **Confirm tier + setup** — 🟢/🟡 only proceed. Read tools, time, parts, savings:
   *"10mm socket, 25 min, Bosch AC cabin filter ₹350–700. Service-centre ₹1 200 — aap
   ₹400 bacha loge."*
3. **Pre-flight safety** (spoken first, every time): engine off, key out, handbrake on,
   wheels chocked, **engine cool** (for anything near coolant/exhaust), gloves/eye care.
4. **One step at a time** — speak a step → *"ho gaya? say HAAN ya tap"* → next step.
   Never dump all steps at once (blind/illiterate).
5. **Checkpoints** — after each safety-relevant step, confirm it's right before moving on.
6. **Finish + log** — test, log the repair to the [Vehicle Health Passport](../memory/vehicle_health_passport.md),
   offer prevention tip. `fw_diy` widget.

## The allow list (🟢/🟡 — Chitti coaches)
AC cabin filter · engine air filter · wiper blades · bulb/fuse swap (re-blow → stop) ·
tyre pressure · washer-fluid · coolant top-up (cold) · 12V terminal clean · easy-access
spark-plug swap.

## NEVER coached (🟠/🔴 — hard stop)
Airbag/SRS · ABS hydraulics/module · brake lines/bleed · fuel injector rail · timing
belt (interference) · EV high-voltage / orange cables · AC refrigerant · suspension
strut/spring · steering rack · head gasket/bore ([../guardrails/diy-safety.md](../guardrails/diy-safety.md)).

## Hard rules
- Safety tier caps DIY — no fix above the ceiling, ever, no matter the cost saving.
- A 👎 *"DIY se kharab ho gaya"* → P0 same-day review; re-grade the tier.

## Accessibility
Every step spoken + captioned + ISL; "say HAAN or tap" to advance (mute-safe); picture
of each step for illiterate drivers; safety setup always spoken first for blind drivers.
