# SOP — Scam Quote Check (Scam Shield playbook)

**Trigger:** *"service centre ne AC compressor ke liye ₹35 000 maanga — theek hai?"*

## Steps
1. **Get the item + car** — what work, which car (brand/model/fuel — the band depends on
   all). Ask if unclear; never assume an expensive item.
2. **Pull the fair band** — [Cost Agent](../swarm/cost-agent.md) → parts-only +
   parts+labour band ([MECHANIC_KNOWLEDGE §6](../skills/MECHANIC_KNOWLEDGE.md)) + city delta.
3. **Place the quote** —
   | Position | Symbol + word |
   |---|---|
   | Inside band | ✅ "theek hai — fair range mein" |
   | A bit above | ⚠️ "thoda upar — pooch sakte ho" |
   | Well above | 🚩 "fair band se kaafi upar lag raha" |
4. **Empower, don't accuse** — *"AC compressor ka fair range ₹18 000–24 000 hai;
   ₹35 000 us se kaafi upar. Pehle gas + cabin filter check karwao — ho sakta hai
   compressor badalne ki zaroorat hi na ho. Genuine OEM part hai? Labour kitna?"*
   **Never** "yeh centre loot raha hai" or name-and-accuse
   ([../guardrails/scam-shield-rules.md](../guardrails/scam-shield-rules.md)).
5. **Question the need too** — if the diagnosis behind the quote is uncertain, flag
   that the **work itself** may not be needed: *"pehle confirm karo yeh kaam zaroori hai."*
   Catch the classic AC-compressor / clutch-overhaul / "whole-assembly" up-sell.
6. **AMC / warranty** — if the car is under AMC/warranty, *"yeh cover hona chahiye —
   claim number aur written reason maango."*
7. **Per-response widget** — `fw_scam_shield`.

## Hard rules
- Bands, never one "correct" price ([../guardrails/never-claim-certainty.md](../guardrails/never-claim-certainty.md)).
- Never defame a named mechanic/centre — judge the **quote vs band**, not the person.
- Honesty score is community-aggregate + anonymised, never a public accusation.

## Accessibility
Band + verdict spoken (blind), symbol+word (deaf, never colour-only), tap to ask
"what should I say to the service centre?" (mute/illiterate get a spoken script).
