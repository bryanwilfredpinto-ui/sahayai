# PRODUCT_VISION — Chitti Car Doctor

## Mission (the one sentence)

> **"My car has a problem — do I really need a service centre, can I fix it
> myself, and is this quote fair?"**

Chitti Car Doctor answers that question, by voice, for every 4-wheeler owner in
Bharat — before they drive to a workshop, before they hand over ₹35 000.

NOT: *"Book a service."* NOT: *"Sell a spare part."* NOT: *"Drive engagement."*

## The problem we are solving

India has tens of millions of cars — the Swift, the Creta, the Nexon are the
aspiration and the livelihood of the rising middle class. Yet the owner is the
most information-poor person in the transaction, and the stakes are **far bigger
than a bike**:

- **Overcharged — big.** *"AC compressor ₹35 000"* when the fair band is ₹18-24k,
  and the real fault is a ₹2 000 gas top-up. *"Full clutch overhaul ₹45 000"* when
  the cable just needed adjusting. The owner has no way to check.
- **Scammed.** A ₹2 000 sensor sold as an "ECU replacement, ₹40 000." Fake brake
  pads and fake oil filters that *kill a whole family* at highway speed.
- **Unaware.** Doesn't know the check-engine light means a misfire that will
  destroy the catalytic converter (₹15-50k) if ignored; doesn't know the diesel
  DPF needs a regen run; doesn't know the PUC expired (₹10 000 fine).
- **Unable to explain.** "Awaaz aa rahi hai" — but which awaaz? "Light jal raha hai"
  — but which light? The owner cannot describe a symptom in the mechanic's
  language, so the mechanic decides the price.
- **Range-anxious (EV).** The Tata Nexon EV owner doesn't know if a short range is
  the AC, the cold, the terrain, or a real battery State-of-Health problem.

Chitti inverts this. Its first move is never "go to the service centre." It is:
**"Bataao kya ho raha hai — main dekhta hoon."** ("Tell me what's happening — let
me look.")

## Two modes — works for everyone, advanced for nearly every car

### Mode 1 — No External Device
Needs nothing but the owner's phone:

- **Voice** — describe the symptom in Hinglish / any of 26 languages.
- **Video / photo** — show the coolant puddle, the worn pad, the smoke colour.
- **Dashboard photo** — snap the warning lights (check-engine, ABS, battery, oil,
  coolant temp); Chitti reads them.
- **Sound** — record a 10-second clip of the engine; Chitti ranks candidate faults.

Every Swift, Creta, Nexon, Baleno and Venue owner is served on day one.

### Mode 2 — OBD2 / Bluetooth ELM327 — FIRST-CLASS for cars
**This is where Chitti Car Doctor shines.** Unlike bikes, **every car sold in India
since 2010 has a standard OBD-II port**. A ₹400-700 ELM327 Bluetooth / CAN reader
turns the phone into a full diagnostic scanner:

- **Standard DTC P-code library** — the OBD-II generic P-codes are *standardised
  across every manufacturer*. P0301 means "cylinder 1 misfire" on a Swift, a Creta
  and a Nexon alike. Chitti decodes every code into Hinglish + a ₹ cost band.
- **Live data** — coolant temp, RPM, fuel-trim (short + long), MAF, throttle,
  battery voltage, fuel level, intake temp, vehicle speed, O2 sensors — streamed live.
- **Freeze-frame** — the snapshot the ECU captured *at the moment the fault tripped*
  — the single most powerful clue a mechanic uses, now in the owner's hand.
- **Read & clear MIL** — read the check-engine light, understand it, decide before
  paying anyone to "plug in the scanner" (often a ₹500-1000 charge by itself).

Mode 2 is the **car's superpower**. For bikes it was a niche power-up; for cars it
is mainstream — the killer feature that puts FIXD / Torque Pro / Car Scanner depth
in the owner's pocket, in Hinglish, voice-first, for the four users.

## The moat — what no app has built

1. **Accessibility-first, voice-first** — a blind owner says "Chitti, mera
   dashboard padho" and hears every warning light. No FIXD-for-mechanics does this.
2. **DIY coaching** — not "go to a mechanic" but "yahan, ye karo" with tools, time,
   difficulty and a video — when and only when it is *safe* (never brake/airbag/HV-EV).
3. **Scam / overcharge detection** — upload the quote or invoice; Chitti returns
   the fair band and a Fair / High / Scam verdict by city. Most valuable here —
   car bills are 5-20× a bike's.
4. **Vehicle memory twin** — Chitti remembers your model, fuel type, service
   history, tyre age, battery age, brake age, and *predicts* what fails next.
5. **Preventive maintenance** — weather-aware, odometer-aware reminders before the
   part fails, not after.
6. **Sound diagnosis** — diagnose the awaaz the owner cannot describe.
7. **Swarm diagnosis** — 8 specialist agents vote; the owner sees a calibrated
   *"Misfire 80% / Coil 12% / Fuel 8%"*, never a false certainty.
8. **OBD2 freeze-frame + live PIDs in Hinglish** — the mechanic's own data, owned by the owner.

## What Chitti Car Doctor IS

- A **digital mechanic companion** that translates the car's symptoms (and its
  OBD2 codes) into the owner's language and the service centre's quote into a
  fair-price verdict.
- A **teacher** — it explains *why* the fault happened, *how* severe it is,
  *whether* you can drive, and *what* it should cost.
- A **swarm of eight specialists** that vote before any diagnosis is shown.
- A **voice-first** companion usable end-to-end with zero reading.
- A **vehicle memory** that knows your specific car and predicts its next failure.

## What Chitti Car Doctor is NOT

- **Not a workshop booking funnel.** It never takes a cut of a service. Its success
  is measured in money the owner *kept*, not bookings it generated.
- **Not a parts shop.** It holds no inventory, sells no spare, earns no margin.
- **Not a certainty machine.** It never says "this is definitely the problem." It
  speaks in Likely / Possible and High / Medium / Low confidence, always.
- **Not a mechanic replacement.** For Professional-Required and Emergency jobs it
  routes the owner to a human — and arms them with the fair price first.
- **Not a roadworthiness/fitness certificate.** It never declares a used car "perfect."
