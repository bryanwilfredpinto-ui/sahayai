- **Honesty (SOP 12):** report copy avoids forbidden phrases ("guaranteed job", "100% placement", "you will earn ₹X"). Tested by a forbidden-phrase scan.
- **Privacy:** parsing stays regex-only; tools/profile localStorage-only (UI); "Chitti forget" wipes (UI).
