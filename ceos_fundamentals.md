## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Fundamentals is India's voice-first stock fundamentals assistant for Nifty 500 companies. A user asks "Reliance theek hai lene ke liye?" — Chitti analyses PE ratio, debt/equity, ROE, promoter holding, earnings growth, and gives a plain-Hindi fundamental summary with a value/growth/quality score. Always with "SEBI-registered advisor se confirm karein" disclaimer.

**Core Insight:** India has 8+ crore demat accounts. Most retail investors buy on tips or news. Chitti Fundamentals teaches them to look at PE, debt, ROE, and earnings before buying — in their language.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the trusted investment educator who explains every stock's fundamentals in plain Hindi — PE kya hai, debt kitna hai, promoter holding kitna — and teaches value investing principles to every Indian investor."
