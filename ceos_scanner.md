## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Scanner lets any Indian scan a food packet, medicine, cosmetic, or product — and instantly know: Is it safe? What are the ingredients? Is it FSSAI approved? What are the allergens? Is there a cheaper Jan Aushadhi alternative? In their language, by voice, for free.

**Core Insight:** Millions of Indians buy packaged food without understanding labels — high sodium, hidden sugar, artificial colours, allergens. A voice-first scanner that reads labels in Hindi saves lives and money.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the trusted food inspector in every Indian's pocket — who reads every label, flags every danger, finds every cheaper alternative, and speaks in your language."
