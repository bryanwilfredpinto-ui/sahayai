# SALES_BRIEF — Chitti Vaani

The pitch in pain-and-benefit form. For Bharat, not for SaaS buyers.

## 10 pain points Vaani solves

1. **Blind user cannot send WhatsApp.** The send button is an unlabelled icon that TalkBack reads as "button". The recipient picker is a visual grid.
2. **Mute user cannot dial in an emergency.** Phone trees, IVR menus, and the human on the other side all expect a voice. Typing is too slow when a parent is on the floor.
3. **Elderly user fears locking the screen.** A wrong pattern attempt locks them out for hours. The phone becomes a brick at the worst time.
4. **Illiterate user cannot read an OAuth toggle screen.** "Allow Chitti to send mail on your behalf — Yes / No" is two unreadable words.
5. **Deaf user cannot hear an emergency call ring.** A spouse calling from a hospital reaches a silent phone.
6. **Rural user cannot read the medicine label.** Generic vs brand, dosage, expiry — all in 6-point English.
7. **Blind user cannot verify a UPI recipient.** The VPA is a string of letters that may or may not be the right shop.
8. **Mute user cannot answer "are you OK?"** at a hospital reception. They are turned away because they "won't talk".
9. **Elderly user cannot reach their son at midnight.** They scroll the wrong contact, hit the wrong call button, give up.
10. **Illiterate user cannot read a government scheme PDF.** Eligibility lives behind a 30-page form they cannot parse.

## 10 benefits Vaani delivers

1. **WhatsApp send via voice + readback + 2 s cancel** — blind user is autonomous.
2. **Chitti speaks on the mute user's behalf** — outbound call opens *"I am Chitti, an AI assistant for [name]."*
3. **Phone lock is voice-only, hard-refused-unlock** — elderly user trusts the screen to stay open until they ask.
4. **OAuth flow is narrated end-to-end in the user's language**, lands on the same page on success — illiterate user finishes without reading.
5. **Emergency cascade rings paired Chittis on STREAM_ALARM** — the deaf user's family wakes up.
6. **MedUPI + Vaani read the medicine bill aloud** — rural user hears the generic alternative + Jan Aushadhi price.
7. **Vaani reads back the UPI recipient name** before opening the deep-link — blind user catches typos.
8. **Vaani holds the conversation at hospital reception** — mute user is taken seriously.
9. **Trusted-circle pairing + one big haptic button** — elderly user reaches the right person on the first try.
10. **Chitti Government narrates eligibility in plain Hindi** — illiterate user knows whether to apply, before any form.

Distinguishing claim: Vaani is a **guardian, a commando, a coach** — not a polite assistant. Per `feedback_design_from_pwd_user_perspective.md`.
