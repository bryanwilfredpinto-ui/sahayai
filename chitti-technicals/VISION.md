## The arc in one line

> **First we make the market *legible*. Then we make it *safe*. Then the community makes each other *wiser*.**

Three horizons, in strict order. We do not skip to protection before literacy works, and we do not open the community swarm before protection is trustworthy.

---

## Horizon 1 — Literacy (the read everyone can understand)

**Goal:** every Indian who holds or is pitched a share can find out, in their language, by voice, what the chart actually says — and what it does *not* promise.

- The deterministic engine ([`chitti_technical_engine.js`](../chitti_technical_engine.js)) speaks RSI/MACD/Bollinger/ATR/Roshan/confluence in plain vernacular prose, four-channel, with proper-nouns kept English (Art. 9).
- The blind investor hears the verdict, hears the sonified price line, and can pull the data as a table — a thing no competitor does for stocks (BO2).
- The "most short-term traders lose — SEBI" rail is on *every* verdict from day one. Literacy that omits this is mis-education.
- **Success looks like:** a Telugu-only senior understands her PSU bank share's read and gives it a 👍 ([SUCCESS_METRICS.md](SUCCESS_METRICS.md) North Star — comprehension).

## Horizon 2 — Protection (the guardian)

**Goal:** the same user who now *understands* the market is *defended* in it.

- **Anti-scam Tip Shield** (BO8) graduates from a deterministic pattern check into the strongest moat: forwarded "tips" are screened for guaranteed-returns / pump language / unregistered-advisor / urgency, and cross-linked to Chitti UPI and Chitti Legal for reporting.
- **Dual journal + honest AI insights** (BO9): after 10 paper trades, Chitti reflects back overtrading, cutting-winners, emotional-trading — truthfully, winners *and* losers. Loss-spiral cool-downs and crisis→Tele-MANAS 14416 (no LLM) make protection a duty of care, not a feature.
- **Senior-citizen shield:** the cold-called parent is the design center of Horizon 2 — the person the whole industry leaves exposed.
- **Success looks like:** scam tips caught before money moves; paper-trade-before-live ratio rising; zero real orders ever placed.

## Horizon 3 — Community (the swarm makes everyone wiser)

**Goal:** every instance of Chitti Technicals learns from every other — *anonymised, user-owned, never sold* — so the whole community gets safer together (SAHAYAI_MASTER §2f Swarm; user-owned camera intelligence §2b).

- Anonymised patterns (high 👍 reads, 👎→👍 reversals, recurring scam-tip signatures) are detected, validated (≥100 confirmations), and — for this MEDIUM/HIGH-risk financial domain — **human-reviewed** before any push to `skills/*.md`.
- Locked decisions are **never** learnable: the swarm cannot teach Chitti to push a trade, place an order, or drop the SEBI rail.
- An annual honest community report (akin to the camera-intelligence FSSAI pattern) surfaces aggregate scam-tip trends to protect the next user.
- **COMING SOON, honestly labelled** until built — never a fake demo.
