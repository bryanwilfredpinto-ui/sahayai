# PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION — should this product exist?

> Produced under the Master Product Validation Rule **before** any CEOS/code. Author wore five hats: Founder · CTO · Product Architect · Venture Capitalist · Devil's Advocate · Accessibility Expert. The job was to **kill the idea**, not to build it.

## Verdict (one line)

- ❌ **Naive "buy/sell signals for everyone" → ~58/100 → KILL.** Violates the Founder Rule: it measurably *harms* financial wellbeing (SEBI: 9/10 traders lose).
- ✅ **Re-scoped "honest vernacular technical-literacy + anti-scam guardian for people already holding or being pitched shares; analysis, never advice; paper-only" → 82/100 → BUILD.**
- **The re-scope is the condition of approval and is law in [CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md).**

## Phase 1 — Problem (80/100)
- **Problem:** ~15 cr demat holders (post-2020), heavily semi-literate + vernacular + first-time, plus their seniors being cold-called with tips, cannot tell if a chart/tip is real or a scam — and every tool assumes sight + English.
- **Pain:** severe + measured (SEBI: ~70% intraday lose; 9/10 F&O lose avg ₹1.1 L; rampant targeted tip-fraud).
- **Honest caveat:** NOT a top-10 problem for the bottom-of-pyramid illiterate farmer (food/health/debt come first). It IS for the semi-literate first-time investor + senior parent. **Build for them, not a fantasy farmer-day-trader.**
- **Pillars improved:** Financial Wellbeing + Safety (anti-scam) + Accessibility — *only when scoped as protection.*

## Phase 2 — Competition (88/100)
20 global + 20 India audited ([RESEARCH_BEST_APPS.md](RESEARCH_BEST_APPS.md)). Whole industry converged on the silent Strong-Buy→Strong-Sell gauge. **Not one of 40** speaks the verdict, explains in vernacular voice, serves the four users, or frames as anti-scam. Clear whitespace.

## Phase 3 — Accessibility advantage (90/100)
Decisive and unique — the strongest leg. The blind investor gets *nothing* from any competitor. No 20-point penalty (accessibility IS the major advantage).

## Phase 4 / 7 — Devil's advocate (survived)
20 kill-shots fired. The fatal ones (it's speculation; predatory to the vulnerable; fantasy persona; SEBI-unregistered; commodity gauge) **all die under the re-scope** — guardian/literacy/anti-scam, paper-only, analysis-not-advice. Real constraints remain (no direct revenue; incumbents *could* copy tech) but none are killers because incumbents **structurally won't serve this segment** — that neglect is the moat.
