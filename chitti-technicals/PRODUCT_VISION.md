## The picture

A 58-year-old retired schoolteacher in Warangal holds 40 shares of a PSU bank. Her son's friend forwards her a WhatsApp message: *"Guaranteed 3x in 2 weeks, buy now, limited tip."* She cannot read English finance jargon. She cannot read a candlestick chart. Every app on her phone assumes she can do both, and most of them want her to trade more.

She opens nothing. She just **asks Vaani**, in Telugu, by voice: *"Is this tip real? What is my bank share actually doing?"*

Chitti Technicals answers — in Telugu, by voice — in plain words:
- *"This message has the marks of a scam. Chitti is not telling you to buy. 'Guaranteed returns' is illegal in India; no honest advisor promises this."*
- *"Your bank share: the chart is sideways. RSI is 48 — neither overbought nor oversold. Most short-term traders lose money — SEBI says 9 of 10 lose. Here is what the chart says; be careful."*

She *understood the market*. No other app on earth did that for her — not in her language, not by voice, not as a guardian instead of a croupier.

**That is the wardrobe moment.** Just as a wardrobe app should tell you honestly what to wear for *your* body, *your* day, *your* budget — Chitti Technicals tells you honestly what the market is doing for *your* holding, *your* ability, *your* language. Not what makes it money. What is true.

## Who we build for (and who we refuse to pretend we serve)

We build for the **real buyer**: the semi-literate, vernacular, first-time investor who already holds shares — and his senior-citizen parent who is being cold-called and WhatsApp-pitched with tips. (See [PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION.md](PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION.md) Phase 1.)

We do **not** build for a fantasy: the illiterate farmer-day-trader. For the bottom-of-pyramid illiterate user, food, health, and debt come first — and pretending otherwise to justify a trading app is exactly the predation we exist to oppose. We are honest about who we help.

The four-user contract (blind / deaf / mute / illiterate) is the **floor**, not the ceiling — every one of them must complete every journey. Above the floor, the Disability Profile personalises (low-vision, elderly, cognitive, motor, rural).

## The inversion (the one idea that defines us)

> **Most trading apps want you to trade more. Chitti Technicals wants you to lose less.**

Every existing platform's incentive is engagement → more trades → more brokerage. That incentive, pointed at blind/illiterate/poor users and called "accessibility," is predatory. We invert it:

| The industry default | The Chitti Technicals inversion |
|---|---|
| A silent Strong-Buy → Strong-Sell needle | A spoken, cited, four-channel honest read |
| "Trade more, trade now, limited time" | "Most short-term traders lose — be careful, Sire" |
| Signals = the product | **Understanding + protection** = the product |
| Tap to execute | Paper journal only; Chitti never places an order |
| A fabricated "92% accurate" badge | Banded, reproducible confidence; zero fabricated % |
| Sight + English assumed | Voice + 26 languages; blind user served first |

**Lose less, not trade more.** This is why Chitti Technicals deserves to exist. It is the condition of approval ([PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION.md](PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION.md), 82/100) and it is law ([CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md) Founder Rule).
