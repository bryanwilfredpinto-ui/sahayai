## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti News is India's voice-first accessible news assistant. It reads today's top headlines in the user's language and state, summarises each story in 3 bullets ("Chitti's Take"), fact-checks viral claims, and reads everything aloud for blind and illiterate users. No algorithm. No clickbait. No paid promotion.

**Core Insight:** 250M+ Indians can hear but not read. AIR news is available but dry. Social media news is fast but unreliable. Chitti News is the trusted middle ground — curated, summarised, voice-first, in 26 languages.
