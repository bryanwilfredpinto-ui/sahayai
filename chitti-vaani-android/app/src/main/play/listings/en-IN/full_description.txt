NIGHT MODE — DESIGNED FOR THE ELDERLY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Between 10 PM and 6 AM, Chitti silences your phone so elderly parents can sleep undisturbed. When a call arrives:
• Chitti speaks: "[Caller] is calling. Say haan to answer."
• 10-second window to respond with your voice
• If no response, Chitti answers and checks if it is an emergency
• Emergency keyword detected → loud alarm, vibration, wakes the user
• Family is notified. Cops are NEVER auto-dialed.
