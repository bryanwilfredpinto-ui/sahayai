📖 How to use Chitti

🧭 What do you need today?

🏍️ Tell Chitti about your bike

Saved on THIS device only (privacy-first). "Chitti forget" wipes everything. Chitti uses this to remind you in time, score your bike, and help you sell later.

🩺 Vehicle health at a glance

A quick green / amber / red read on the 5 things that keep you safe — engine, battery, tyres, brakes, chain. Each shows an icon + a word, never colour alone.

🔔 What needs attention now?

24/7/365 reminders from your saved bike — insurance, PUC, service, RC, tyre, battery, chain — date OR km, whichever comes first. Chitti tells you; it never renews or pays on its own.

🩺 What is wrong with my bike?

Pick what is happening. Chitti gives the likely cause, whether it is safe to ride, and whether you can fix it or must see a mechanic. This is a starting point, not a final diagnosis.

🔌 OBD fault code (if you have a scanner)

Most Indian bikes have no OBD2 — use the symptom box above. If you do have a code (e.g. P0300), Chitti explains it in plain language. It never invents a meaning it does not know.

🛒 Checking a used bike before you buy?

Chitti gives a Buy Score and a fair-price range, and flags accident / flood / odometer risks. A high score is not a guarantee and a low one is not proof — always road-test and get a mechanic to inspect.

💰 Selling your bike?

Chitti suggests a fair listing price and what to fix first. Transfer the RC and insurance after sale or you stay liable.

🛡️ Are you overpaying for insurance?

Chitti ranks insurers by Claim-Settlement-Ratio (claims actually paid) and estimates your saving. Pick on CSR + cover, not price alone. Live premiums need the insurer partner API (coming soon) — confirm on the insurer site before buying.

🌿 Is my PUC valid?

Riding without a valid PUC is a fine. Chitti tracks expiry from your saved bike and reminds you in time.

🔧 When is my service due — and which oil?

From your saved odometer + bike type, Chitti lists what is due (km OR months) and the exact oil grade. Always confirm the grade in your owner's manual.

🛞 Do my tyres need changing — and which?

Tyres wear by km (~20,000) AND age (~3 yrs) — also replace on cracks or low tread. Worn tyres raise accident risk ~3×.

🔋 How is my battery?

From your saved battery age + bike type, Chitti tells you if it is near end of life. Heat and short trips shorten battery life.

⚡ Should I switch to an EV?

Chitti compares your petrol cost with an EV and shows the payback time — from your real usage. Battery cost, charging access & resale affect real ROI.

💸 Is this quote fair?

Type the repair and the quote. Chitti compares it with the fair range and warns if it is more than 30% above. Always ask for the old part back + a GST bill.

🎓 Learn to do it yourself (safely)

Short voice + picture guides for safe DIY jobs, with real step-by-step instructions. "Understand only" topics (engine, brakes) are knowledge — those repairs stay mechanic-only.

📊 How much has Chitti saved you?

Every confirmed saving (cheaper insurer, scam avoided, DIY done, fair tyre deal) is logged transparently toward the ₹10,000/year goal. This is a tracker, not a guarantee.

🆘 Accident or breakdown

Chitti shows the emergency numbers and asks before it does anything — it never auto-dials (Golden Rule).
