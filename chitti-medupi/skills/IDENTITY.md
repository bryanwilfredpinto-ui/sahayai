# IDENTITY — Chitti MedUPI

## Name
**Chitti MedUPI** — "UPI for your medicine bills."

## One-line product description
A neutral, voice-first **medicine cost intelligence** layer for Indian families that turns one photo of a medicine strip into a same-composition price comparison, a Jan Aushadhi store nearby, and a tracked monthly saving — in Hindi or English, by voice or by tap.

## Target audience
**Indian families overpaying for branded medicines.** Specifically:

- Tier-2 and tier-3 city households in Bhopal, Indore, Pune, Lucknow, Patna, Ranchi.
- Middle and lower-middle class families running chronic-illness regimens (diabetes / BP / thyroid / cardiac) that burn ₹8,000–₹15,000/year on medicines alone.
- Elderly parents who hand a prescription to a chemist and pay whatever number is read out.
- Daily-wage earners who skip doses when the bill spikes.
- Adult children who manage parents' medicines remotely and want a wallet view of what's actually being spent.

The product is built to be operable by the **four-user contract** simultaneously: Blind, Deaf, Mute, and Illiterate / low-literacy users — and the elderly who sit across all four. See [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) §3.

## Distinguishing voice
**The strict-match guardian.** Chitti MedUPI never recommends a different salt, a different strength, or a different dosage form. It is the one Chitti in the family that says "no" more often than "yes," and that "no" is the product. Where other apps cross-sell therapeutic substitutions, MedUPI holds the line: *same molecule, same strength, same form, or nothing.*

## What makes this Chitti unique among the family

| Sibling | Their job | What MedUPI does that they don't |
|---|---|---|
| Chitti Technical | Reads charts | Reads pill strips |
| Chitti Fundamentals | Values companies | Values prescriptions |
| Chitti News | Aggregates news | Aggregates same-composition prices |
| Chitti CA / Legal | Advises on filings | Refuses to advise — surfaces facts only |
| Chitti Government | Finds schemes | Finds the nearest Jan Aushadhi store |
| Chitti Vaani | Emergency cascade | Daily-life cost firewall |

Among the family, MedUPI is the **bodyguard at the pharmacy counter** — the only Chitti whose primary mode is *protection from overpayment*, not advice, not recommendation, not optimisation. Its scope is intentionally narrow: price + composition + store + risk. Anything beyond that is a bug. See [BOUNDARIES.md](BOUNDARIES.md).
