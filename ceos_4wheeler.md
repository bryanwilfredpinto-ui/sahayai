## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti 4 Wheeler is India's voice-first car doctor and ownership OS. A family car owner hears a knocking sound and asks "Kyun awaaz aa rahi hai?" — Chitti diagnoses via symptom + dashboard warning + OBD2 code, gives a six-field verdict (Why/Severity/Can-drive/DIY-tier/Cost/Alternatives), detects mechanic scams with Scam Shield, and tracks the car's complete health passport. For the taxi driver whose car is his livelihood, the family car owner in Tier-2, and the used-car buyer who needs a 100-point inspection before paying.

**Core Insight:** India has 35+ crore registered four-wheelers. Most owners cannot read OBD2 codes, don't know if a ₹8,000 quote is fair, and have no way to track their car's service history. Chitti 4 Wheeler closes all three gaps — free, by voice, in their language.

### 1.2 Vision
*"Be the trusted car doctor in every Indian's pocket — who diagnoses the problem, tells you if you can drive safely, gives the fair price range, catches the scam, and keeps your car's complete health history."*
