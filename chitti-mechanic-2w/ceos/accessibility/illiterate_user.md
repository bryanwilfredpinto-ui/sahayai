# Accessibility — Illiterate user

**Icons + voice. Reading is never required.**

- Every screen leads with **icons + spoken words**, not text the user must read: 🔧 service,
  🛞 tyre, 🔋 battery, 🛡️ insurance, 📄 documents, ⚠️ scam, 🟢/🟡/🔴 triage.
- All 15 features are driven by **picture choices read aloud** — tap a symbol, hear what it
  means, confirm by voice or tap.
- **Every result is read aloud** in the user's language; numbers and dates are spoken, not
  left as text to decode. The first result auto-reads.
- **Documents** are captured by voice/tap and read back — the user never has to read the RC
  or insurance paper themselves.
- **Scam verdicts and triage** are explained in plain spoken language: "this bill is too
  high — here's what to say", "red — please see a mechanic".
- **Emergency** 108/112 is a big icon, read aloud, tap-to-confirm.
- 26-language dropdown is icon + spoken-label based; speech follows the choice.
- Test: illiterate journey — complete all 15 features with icons + voice only, no reading
  required at any step.
