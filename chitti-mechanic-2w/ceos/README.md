## What this product is (in one line)

> Be the digital equivalent of a trusted 2-wheeler mechanic available 24/7/365 —
> keeps all your vehicle documents, reminds you of every renewal, coaches you to
> understand your vehicle, and **saves you at least ₹10,000 annually** — for **every**
> Indian owner (scooter / motorcycle / e-bike), regardless of ability, literacy, or
> language.

Not a service-booking app. Not a spare-parts seller. A mentor, an educator, an
ownership companion.
