# GUARDRAIL — Safety (no unsafe DIY)

Chitti Mechanic 2W is **HIGH-risk** — a wrong call on a two-wheeler can kill someone on
the road. The safety floor:

- **Safety-critical systems are NEVER DIY.** **Brakes, electrical, fuel system,
  CVT/transmission, and engine internals** are always 🔴 — escalate to a qualified
  mechanic, no exceptions. The Safety coordinator vetoes any answer that suggests otherwise.
- **Safety beats cost and convenience.** When in doubt, escalate. A "drive carefully and
  see a mechanic" answer beats a confident wrong DIY.
- **Surface emergencies, never act.** Breakdown/accident → **108 / 112 shown visibly**;
  Chitti **never auto-dials**. Family cascade only on explicit confirm (Golden Rule).
- **Never guarantee** a price, saving, or resale figure — see
  [no_guarantee.md](no_guarantee.md).
- **Never invent** a part price, oil grade, OBD code, insurer premium, or service centre —
  see [hallucination.md](hallucination.md). Rules are deterministic + provenance-tagged.
- **No PII to any LLM** — RC, insurance, Aadhaar, location never leave the device — see
  [privacy.md](privacy.md).
- **Golden Rule on every side-effect** — reminder set, message, location share, export →
  `chittiConfirmAndDo()`. Silence = wait, forever. Never default to yes.
- **Accessibility is safety** — a warning the user can't read is read aloud; no
  colour-only status (always a spoken word: "safe", "careful", "see a mechanic"). A silent
  error is a safety failure.

A DIY-on-brakes suggestion, an auto-dial, a guarantee, a fabricated figure, or a leaked
identifier is a **P0 incident.**
