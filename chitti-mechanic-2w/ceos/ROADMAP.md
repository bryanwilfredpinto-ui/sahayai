## Build Orders (BO1 – BO10)

### BO1 — Document Vault
RC, insurance, PUC, DL, service bills, warranty cards — stored **local-only**,
"Chitti forget" deletes all. Voice-read every document; icon-driven for illiterate
users. *Foundation of the Vehicle Twin.* (Article 4)

### BO2 — Smart Reminders
Track and remind for insurance, PUC, service, RC, tyre, battery, chain. Deterministic
due-date math; multi-modal alerts (shown + spoken + buzzed); offline-capable; SMS
fallback for rural users. (Article 5)

### BO3 — Insurance Comparison
Compare **8+ insurers**, show real rupee savings, never hide the cheapest honest
option. Mandatory, not optional. Saving line-itemed into the Savings Tracker.
(Article 7)

### BO4 — PUC + Service
PUC due-date intelligence + fine avoidance; periodic service scheduling by model and
usage; service-interval rules grounded in the Indian market. (Articles 5, 11)

### BO5 — Tyre + Battery
Tyre and battery recommendations by model, usage, and budget — free/adequate option
first, premium option with honest trade-off. Replacement-due reminders. (Article 6)

### BO6 — Buy / Sell
Fair used-bike valuation for buyer and seller; inspection checklist; basic history /
fraud signals. Serves the used-bike-buyer persona. (Article 6)

### BO7 — Diagnostics + Scam
Symptom-based diagnostics with **safety triage** (🟢/🟡/🔴); Scam Shield for fake
insurance, inflated quotes, counterfeit parts, odometer fraud. Target ≥80% detection.
(Articles 8, and Scam Shield)

### BO8 — Education + AI Coach
Ownership education that **teaches, not just predicts** (Founder Rule: Prediction →
Education). DeepSeek narration of symptoms and fixes in the user's language, grounded
by the deterministic engine. (Platform lock: DeepSeek-only)

### BO9 — Vehicle Twin + Savings
Lifelong on-device ownership memory; journals every service, part, fuel fill, and
reminder acted on; Savings Tracker line-items toward the **≥₹10,000** annual goal,
honestly, as a range. (Articles 9, 10)

### BO10 — Accessibility + Certification
Full 9/9 archetype certification, 26/26 languages, Visual+Audio+Haptic on every
output, @375px, offline. The release gate from [SUCCESS_METRICS.md](SUCCESS_METRICS.md)
must pass end-to-end. (Article 1)

## Sequencing rule

One BO at a time. Each BO is researched (top reference apps + Indian-market grounding),
built to full feature surface, then certified against all nine archetypes and 26
languages before the next BO starts. Iterating a half-built BO to "comprehensive" over
several turns is a defect, not progress.
