## Why it scores BUILD

- **Genuine, large market gap.** No Indian app gives the blind, deaf, mute,
  illiterate, elderly or rural 2-wheeler owner a trusted, voice-first ownership OS.
  Existing apps book services or sell parts — none coach, none keep documents
  privately, none compare insurance honestly for this user.
- **Massive addressable base.** Hundreds of millions of Indian 2-wheeler owners
  across scooters, motorcycles and e-bikes; the design-target gig delivery rider
  segment alone is enormous and underserved.
- **Concrete, defensible value.** The **≥₹10,000/year honest savings** thesis is
  built from real, line-itemed sources (insurance ₹1,000–5,000, PUC fines
  ₹1,000–2,000, prevented engine damage ₹5,000–10,000) — not a marketing number.
- **Accessibility-first moat.** Nine archetypes + 26 languages + Visual/Audio/Haptic
  is a moat competitors cannot cheaply copy.
- **Deterministic, auditable core.** Reminder math, savings tallies, triage and
  insurance comparison are rule-based and reproducible — trustworthy by construction.
- **Platform fit.** Sits cleanly under the SAHAYAI platform locks: DeepSeek-only,
  Vaani-as-sole-interface, Golden Rule confirm-before-act, family-cascade emergency,
  honest stubs over fake demos.

## Honest risks weighed in the score

- **Live data dependencies are partner-gated.** mParivahan / DigiLocker integration
  needs approved government API access — shipped as honest COMING SOON until then.
- **Hardware features deferred.** OBD-dongle reads need hardware integration — honest
  stub for now.
- **Savings must never be guaranteed.** The headline ₹10,000 is a tracked goal shown
  as a range — guaranteeing it would be a P0 incident ([CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md)
  Article 9). The justification accounts for this discipline.
- **Safety liability.** DIY triage carries real-world safety stakes — mitigated by the
  zero-tolerance 🟢/🟡/🔴 triage rule and "escalate the colour when in doubt."
