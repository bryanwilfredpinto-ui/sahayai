**Pattern of what the field misses:** AI products either hallucinate numbers or hide
their reasoning. Chitti's answer is that **the rules are the product** — the LLM may
only narrate a number the deterministic engine already computed and provenance-tagged,
and it must say "I'm not sure" rather than invent.
