**World Class Chitti Health Scanner — Commando Discipline. Zero Excuses.**

# COSDF Level 2 — PERSONAS

> Brand palette — Saffron `#FF9933` · Navy `#000080` · Green `#138808`
> Every persona is served under the **four-user accessibility contract**: Blind / Deaf / Mute / Illiterate — Voice IN + Voice OUT + icons/symbols + plain language, never colour-only.

---

## P1 — Rural Farmer

**Region:** Villages, agrarian Bharat — nearest screening often 50 km away.

**Needs:**
- A first health screen at home, without travel or cost.
- Plain-language readout in the local language (no English, no Hinglish).
- Clear "should I travel to a clinic or not?" guidance.
- Tracking of sun / skin / wound changes from field work.

**Accessibility tie-in:** Often **Illiterate** → voice IN + voice OUT + icons; never text-only.

---

## P2 — Parent / Child

**Needs:**
- Quick check of a child's rash, fever-marks, eye redness, or skin change.
- Calm, non-panic explanation — no fear-mongering to a worried parent.
- Clear monitor vs. seek-care guidance for a small child.
- Memory of how the rash changed since yesterday.

**Accessibility tie-in:** Hands-full parent → **Voice IN + Voice OUT**; symbols (🟢/🟡/🔴) for fast read.

---

## P3 — Elderly (65+)

**Needs:**
- Easy capture with large taps and spoken guidance.
- Watching slow changes — skin, nails, eyes, posture.
- Gentle, patient pace; no jargon.
- Escalation that loops in family (Chitti Health File timeline).

**Accessibility tie-in:** Low vision + low literacy → **Voice OUT + large icons**; never colour-only.

---

## P4 — Diabetic

**Needs:**
- Routine **diabetic foot tracking** — daily / weekly self-scans.
- Early flag on wounds, ulcers, colour/skin change that won't heal.
- Reminders to re-scan (gated through the confirm Golden Rule).
- Timeline showing healing or worsening over time.

**Accessibility tie-in:** Often **Blind / low-vision** from diabetic retinopathy → **voice-guided self-exam**.

---

## P5 — Cancer Survivor

**Needs:**
- Vigilant monitoring of moles, skin spots, and post-treatment changes.
- Honest confidence + "consider consult" without inducing panic.
- A trustworthy memory of every spot over months and years.
- Dignity — no fear-mongering, no shaming.

**Accessibility tie-in:** Anxiety-sensitive → calm **Voice OUT**, plain language, clear escalation path.

---

## P6 — Blind

**Needs:**
- **Blind self-exam** — voice-guided camera positioning ("move a little closer / left").
- Fully spoken plain-language readout of what Chitti notices.
- Independence — self-screen without depending on a sighted person.
- Confirm gate by voice ("Haan / Nahi"), never default-to-yes.

**Accessibility tie-in:** Core of the contract — **Voice IN + Voice OUT**; zero reliance on sight or colour.

---

## P7 — Deaf

**Needs:**
- Full **text + icon** readout of every result (no audio dependency).
- Visual confidence indicators paired with text (🟢 normal / 🟡 monitor / 🔴 seek care).
- ISL-friendly, symbol-rich explanations.
- Tap-based confirm gate (mute-safe, sight-based).

**Accessibility tie-in:** **Visual / text-first**; never audio-only; colour always paired with icon + text.

---

## P8 — Illiterate

**Needs:**
- Entirely **voice + symbol** interface — no reading required.
- Spoken instructions and spoken results in their own language.
- Icons for action: 🟢 monitor / 🟡 consider consult / 🔴 seek care.
- Auto-read on first visit; nothing assumes the ability to read.

**Accessibility tie-in:** **Voice OUT + symbols** is the entire interface; text is supplementary only.

---

## P9 — General Public

**Needs:**
- A trustworthy, private first-look for everyday concerns.
- Honest confidence and clear next steps (monitor / consider consult / seek care).
- Health education — what to look for and how often.
- Affordable onward links (Jan Aushadhi via MedUPI, PMJAY via Government).

**Accessibility tie-in:** Inherits all four-user supports by default — **voice, text, symbols, plain language** always on.

---

> **Every persona, every render:** confidence level + plain-language explanation + suggested action + **"This is not a medical diagnosis."**
>
