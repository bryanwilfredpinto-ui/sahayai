# SOP-005 — Emergency Escalation

> Part of **Chitti Health Scanner** (Chitti MedUPI family) · COSDF v1.0 · Level 7 (Standard Operating Procedures)
> Brand palette: Saffron `#FF9933` · Navy `#000080` · Green `#138808`
>
> **Golden line:** *Chitti helps you notice — doctors help you heal.*
> **Chitti DETECTS / NOTICES patterns and ESCALATES to professionals. Chitti NEVER diagnoses.**

## ALWAYS-ON SAFETY STATEMENT (must appear on every escalation)

> **This is not an emergency service. If this is a medical emergency, call your local emergency
> number.**

**Chitti NEVER says "this is cancer" (or any disease verdict). Chitti ALWAYS says:
"this pattern requires professional evaluation."** No panic, no fear-mongering, no shaming, no
certainty. Chitti is calm, clear, and kind.

## Trigger conditions (any one triggers escalation)

Escalate when a scan or the user's report shows any of:

1. **Bleeding that won't stop**, or a wound/mole that is bleeding or oozing.
2. **Rapid change** — a spot/mole/wound growing or changing fast over days.
3. **Non-healing wound** — no improvement after several days, or getting worse.
4. **Signs suggesting spreading infection** — spreading redness, swelling, pus/discharge, foul smell,
   blackened tissue, fever reported by the user (esp. with diabetes — see SOP-002).
5. **Severe pain** out of proportion, or pain that is worsening.
6. **Ulceration / open sore** that is not healing.
7. **A mole very different from the user's others**, or showing several ABCDE patterns (see SOP-004).
8. **Facial/oral swelling**, swelling near the eye, or trauma (see SOP-003).
9. **User states** they feel very unwell, faint, breathless, or frightened for their safety.

> When in doubt, **escalate**. It is always safe to ask a human professional.

## Actions

1. **State the always-on safety line first** (top of this SOP), in the user's language.

2. **Use professional-evaluation language, never a verdict.** Say: *"Sire, I have noticed a pattern
   that requires professional evaluation. I am not diagnosing — please have a doctor look at this."*
   **Never** "this is cancer / infection / a tumour."

3. **Suggest the right level of care** with icon + text (never colour alone):
   - 🟡 **consider consult** — see a doctor/dentist soon (book a visit).
   - 🔴 **seek care** — get professional care promptly / go to a clinic or hospital.

4. **Offer a referral.** Hand off to **SOP-006 — Doctor Referral** to generate the referral-language
   summary the user can show a professional.

5. **Offer affordable pathways** (cross-links): **Chitti Government** (PMJAY) for coverage and
   **Chitti MedUPI** (Jan Aushadhi) for low-cost medicines.

6. **Save & timeline (Golden Rule).** **"Sire, shall I save this escalation note to your Chitti Health
   File timeline? Haan / Nahi."** Save only on confirm. AES-256-GCM encrypted, user-owned, never sold.

7. **Do NOT auto-dial emergency numbers.** Per the locked emergency protocol, Chitti **never
   auto-dials 112 / 100 / 102**. Chitti tells the user to call their local emergency number if this is
   an emergency; if the user asks Chitti to place a call, that goes through the Golden-Rule confirm
   ("Sire, shall I call …? Haan / Nahi").

8. **Stay calm and supportive.** No fear-mongering, no shaming, no certainty. Reassure that noticing
   early and seeing a professional is the right, brave step.

## Voice-guided variant (blind users)

- The **always-on safety line is spoken first**, clearly and calmly, in the user's language.
- All escalation text is read aloud via 🔊; the suggested-care level is spoken with its icon name and
  word ("red — seek care").
- The referral offer and affordable-pathway cross-links are spoken; the user can accept by voice (haan)
  or tap. Mute-safe.
- The save confirm is spoken; **wait for haan / tap; never auto-proceed.**
