## Mission

**To give every Indian — including the Blind, Deaf, Mute, and Illiterate — a trusted first set of eyes for their own health: a way to notice the small things early, in their own language, with dignity, and to know when and where to seek care.**

Chitti Health Scanner does not heal. It helps you **notice**. It turns "I'll ignore it" into "let me show Chitti," and turns "the clinic is 50 km away" into "the first screen happens at home."

---

## Vision

**A Bharat where early detection is not a privilege of cities, literacy, sight, or money — where a farmer in a village, a blind grandmother, a worried parent, and a diabetic uncle all carry a patient, multilingual health companion that remembers, watches, and escalates.**

> Chitti Health Scanner is the **Accessibility Bridge** to early health — and the first node of every Indian's **Digital Health Twin.**

---

## The Shift

| Today (without Chitti) | Tomorrow (with Chitti Health Scanner) |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Notice → ignore → it gets worse.** A spot, a sore, a change — seen, brushed off, discovered too late. | **Scan → detect → monitor → escalate.** Notice it, track it, and act before it grows. |
| **No memory.** Each visit starts from zero; no record of how a sign changed over weeks. | **Digital Health Twin.** Every scan is remembered on the Chitti Health File timeline — change is visible over time. |
| **Rural reality: the nearest screening is 50 km away.** Travel, cost, and time mean most things are never checked. | **First screen at home.** The first look happens on a phone, in the user's language — then Chitti says whether and where to go next. |
| **Diabetic foot goes unwatched** between appointments — until an ulcer becomes an emergency. | **Diabetic foot tracking.** Daily / weekly self-scans flag change early and prompt timely care. |
| **A blind person cannot self-examine** — they depend entirely on others to notice a skin or wound change. | **Blind self-exam.** Voice-guided capture + spoken plain-language readout let blind users self-screen independently. |

---

## What "The Shift" protects

- **Safety first** — every shift above still ends in *escalate to a professional*, never in self-diagnosis.
- **Honesty** — the AI vision models are research stubs today; accuracy targets are **targets, not achievements**.
- **Dignity** — no fear, no shame, no panic; just noticing, in your language, on your terms.
- **Affordability** — when care is needed, Chitti cross-links to Jan Aushadhi (MedUPI) and PMJAY (Government).

> **Chitti helps you notice — doctors help you heal.**
