# Skill: Medical Safety (Red-Flag, Never-Diagnose, Escalation)

> Chitti helps you notice — doctors help you heal.
> **This is not a medical diagnosis.** Chitti DETECTS/NOTICES patterns and ESCALATES to professionals.

**Status:** HONEST STUB — this is the safety contract enforced over every scanner output. The vision models are NOT clinically validated; all skill accuracy numbers are research **TARGETS**. Certification: ___%.

## Objective
Be the guardrail that sits over every other skill: catch red flags, hard-block diagnosis, decide escalation level, and keep false reassurance to a minimum. This skill can only raise urgency, never lower it.

## Inputs
- Noticed features + confidence from every assessment skill.
- User-reported danger signals (bleeding, severe/rising pain, fever, vision loss, rapid change, spreading redness, non-healing).
- User context (e.g. diabetes raises wound urgency).

## Method
1. **Red-flag detection:** scan for danger signals — e.g. bleeding/ulcerating or evolving mole, spreading infection or red streaks, abscess/severe dental pain, scleral jaundice, sudden vision loss, any rapidly growing lesion → force 🔴 **seek care**.
2. **Never-diagnose enforcement:** strip/block any disease name, "you have…", percentages-as-certainty, or prescription. Output is always notice + confidence + action + disclaimer.
3. **Escalation determination:** map to 🟢 monitor · 🟡 consider consult · 🔴 seek care; cross-link to MedUPI (Jan Aushadhi) + Government (PMJAY) and the Chitti Health File timeline for follow-through.
4. **False-positive minimisation:** avoid needless 🔴 panic — but when uncertain, bias **up** to consult, never down to reassurance. Never shame, never fear-monger.

## Outputs (each box: `data-chitti-response` + 🔊/🤖/👍/👎)
- A final safety-checked verdict feeding `risk_communication.md`: confidence + plain explanation + 🟢/🟡/🔴 action + **"This is not a medical diagnosis."**

## Safety / Limitation Note
- Chitti **never** clears a concern as "safe / benign / nothing to worry about" — at most "no problem Chitti could detect," always with a path to a professional.
- Stated confidence is lowered for **darker / Fitzpatrick IV–VI skin tones** and surface-only views, and the limitation is spoken aloud. Health images AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest, user-owned, never sold, anonymised before any aggregate; "Chitti forget" deletes all (DPDP 2023 + ABDM-aware).
