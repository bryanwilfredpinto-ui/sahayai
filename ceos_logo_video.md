## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Logo & Video Creation is India's voice-first AI design studio. A kirana owner says "Meri dukaan ka logo banana hai — naam hai Sharma General Store" — Chitti creates 5 logo options in 30 seconds. A wedding family says "Shaadi ki reel banana hai" — Chitti creates a 30-second video. All by voice. In 29 languages. Free for basic, nominal for advanced.

**Core Insight:** India has 6+ crore small businesses with no logo, no video, no brand. Design agencies charge ₹5,000-50,000. Chitti charges ₹0 for basic. This is the missing democratisation.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the design studio in every Indian's pocket — that creates logos, videos, and brand materials by voice, in their language, for ₹0 to ₹199."
