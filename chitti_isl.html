COMING SOON · Phase 2

Camera detects your ISL gestures

Point your camera, sign in ISL, and Chitti will interpret and respond — speaking aloud for anyone in the room without an ISL background.

Status: not built. A frame-stream classifier supplier is being evaluated; DeepSeek will handle the language layer.

Why: the same deaf user should be able to talk to Chitti AND have hearing family in the room understand the conversation.

Replace a placeholder with a real ISL video

The same model as voice donations: deaf-community volunteers record short ISL videos for words that today are only emoji-hand placeholders. Each accepted contribution earns a spot in the ISL Hall of Fame.

Status: contribution endpoint not yet live. The architecture is provider-agnostic — chitti_isl_dictionary.json entries swap frames[] for video[] without any frontend change.

⚠️ Not a registered advisor

Chitti is an AI assistant for accessibility and information. It is NOT SEBI registered, NOT a medical practitioner, NOT a legal counsel, and NOT a certified ISL interpreter.

ISL animations on this site are placeholders intended as an honest stub until community-contributed videos replace them. They are not certified ISL signs and must not be used as the sole source for medical, legal, or financial sign interpretation.

For certified ISL interpretation contact ISLRTC.
