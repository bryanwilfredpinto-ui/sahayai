# MASTER PRODUCT CREATION RULE

> **You are NOT allowed to create a product immediately. You must first determine whether the product
> SHOULD exist.** No `CEOS` / `ROLE.md` / `PRD.md` may be written until Phases 1–5 pass with a
> **Build Score ≥ 80 / 100**. This governs every NEW Chitti product (and any "feature" large enough to be
> a product). Applies to Claude (CTO) before any build.

---
## PHASE 1 — PROBLEM VALIDATION
Answer all, with evidence:
1. What exact problem is being solved?
2. Who experiences this problem?
3. How frequently?
4. How painful is it?
5. What are current solutions?
6. Why do current solutions fail?
7. Is this a **top-10 problem** in the user's life?
8. Would users pay attention to it **weekly**?
9. Does it measurably improve at least one of: **Health · Education · Income · Safety · Government Access ·
   Legal Access · Financial Wellbeing · Accessibility**?

**If NO → recommend NOT BUILDING.**

## PHASE 2 — MARKET RESEARCH
Research **Top 20 competitors globally + Top 20 in India**. For each: Strengths · Weaknesses · Missing
features · **Accessibility gaps** · **Trust gaps**. Then answer:
**"What can Chitti do that nobody else does?"**

## PHASE 3 — USER RESEARCH
Create personas — **Student · Homemaker · Farmer · Senior Citizen · Business Owner · Blind · Deaf ·
Illiterate · Rural · Urban**. For each: Pain points · Goals · Frustrations · **Accessibility needs**.

## PHASE 4 — DEVIL'S ADVOCATE (act as an investor trying to KILL it)
1. Why will this fail?
2. Why will users ignore it?
3. Why is it unnecessary?
4. Why will competitors beat it?
5. Why should we NOT build it?
**Only continue if every answer can be overcome.**

## PHASE 5 — PRODUCT JUSTIFICATION → `PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION.md`
Must include: Problem · Users · Market Need · Accessibility Need · Competitive Advantage · Trust Advantage ·
AI Advantage · **Why Chitti Should Exist**. Then give a **Build Score: __ / 100**.
**If below 80 → STOP. Do NOT create the CEOS.**

## PHASE 6 — CEOS CREATION (only after Phases 1–5 pass ≥80)
Then create: ROLE.md · PRODUCT_VISION.md · PERSONAS.md · SUCCESS_METRICS.md · PRD.md · ARCHITECTURE.md ·
SKILLS.md · SOP.md · SWARM.md · GUARDRAILS.md · EVALS.md · OBSERVABILITY.md · ACCESSIBILITY.md · MEMORY.md ·
CERTIFICATION.md · QUALITY_GATES.md.

---
## FOUNDER RULE
Never build features because they sound impressive. Build **only** features that measurably improve:
**Health · Education · Income · Safety · Government Access · Legal Access · Financial Wellbeing · Accessibility.**

> **Trust > Virality · Accessibility > Complexity · Evidence > Assumptions · Research > Opinions · Product > Features.**

---
**Process for a validated product (after ≥80):** then — and only then — the per-BO build loop applies
(research Top 20 apps + Top 20 AI apps per BO → document with best practices → code → test → show →
"is this the best I've ever made?"). See the per-BO rule in the build process.
