Install steps — 5 taps

  1. Open sahayai.in/download.html on your Android phone (or scan the QR code above).
  2. Tap 📥 Download APK.
  3. When Chrome says "This file may be harmful" → tap Download anyway.
  4. Open the APK. Android will say "For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source" → tap Settings, toggle Allow from this source on, go back.
  5. Tap Install. Then Open. Done — Chitti will run you through Device Registration on first launch.

Install करने के 5 step

  1. अपने Android phone पर sahayai.in/download.html खोलिए (या ऊपर का QR code scan कीजिए)।

What Chitti will ask for (and what for)

PermissionWhy
🎙️ MicrophoneVoice IN — speak to Chitti instead of typing. Required.
📞 Phone (CALL_PHONE)"Mom ko call karo" — Chitti dials directly via your SIM. Otherwise the dialer opens pre-filled and you tap green.
💬 SMS (SEND_SMS)"Mom ko SMS karo" — Chitti sends from your SIM. No server cost; your carrier rate applies.
📍 Location (optional)Nearby Chitti business listings (kirana / pharmacy / restaurant). Stays on the device; not uploaded.
🔒 Device Admin (optional)ONLY to lock the phone on your voice command. Chitti can never unlock — Android refuses by design.
🛡️ Accessibility (optional)Auto-tap the WhatsApp send button after your "haan". Scoped strictly to that one button.
🚫 Hard refusalChitti will NEVER auto-dial 112 / 100 / 102 / 108 / 1098 / 1930 / 139. Family-only emergency cascade. Encoded in emergency_service.py.
