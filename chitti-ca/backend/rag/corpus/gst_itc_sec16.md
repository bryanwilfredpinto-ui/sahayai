# Input Tax Credit — eligibility and conditions (CGST Act, Section 16)

A registered person is entitled to take credit of input tax charged on inward
supplies of goods or services used or intended to be used in the course or
furtherance of business, subject to conditions. The credit is credited to the
electronic credit ledger.

Conditions to claim Input Tax Credit (ITC):
- The registered person must possess a tax invoice or debit note issued by a
  registered supplier.
- The details of the invoice must have been furnished by the supplier and must
  appear in the recipient's auto-drafted statement (GSTR-2B) — credit is restricted
  to invoices so communicated.
- The recipient must have received the goods or services.
- The tax charged must have actually been paid to the Government by the supplier.
- The recipient must have furnished the return under Section 39 (e.g. GSTR-3B).

If the recipient does not pay the supplier the value plus tax within 180 days of the
invoice date, the credit taken must be reversed (added to output liability with
interest) and can be re-availed when payment is made.

ITC is NOT available for items blocked under Section 17(5).
