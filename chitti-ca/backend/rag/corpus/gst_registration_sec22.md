# GST registration — threshold and liability (CGST Act, Section 22; with CBIC threshold notifications)

Every supplier is liable to be registered under GST in the State/UT from where they
make a taxable supply of goods or services once their aggregate turnover in a
financial year exceeds the prescribed threshold.

Threshold limits for compulsory registration (aggregate turnover, all-India PAN basis):
- Suppliers of GOODS (normal category States): more than Rs. 40 lakh.
- Suppliers of SERVICES (normal category States): more than Rs. 20 lakh.
- Special category States (and some others): more than Rs. 20 lakh for goods and
  more than Rs. 10 lakh for services.

"Aggregate turnover" includes taxable, exempt, export and inter-State supplies of a
person on the same PAN, computed all-India, but excludes GST itself.

Note: certain persons must register regardless of turnover (Section 24) — for
example persons making inter-State taxable supply, casual taxable persons, persons
required to pay tax under reverse charge, and persons supplying through an
e-commerce operator required to collect TCS.

Always verify the current threshold and your State's category against the official
CGST Act text and the latest CBIC notification at the source URL above.
