# Income-tax return — due dates and late fee (Income-tax Act, Section 139, 234F)

Due date for filing the Income-tax Return (ITR) under Section 139(1) for a normal
financial year, unless extended by the Department:
- 31 July of the assessment year — individuals/HUF and others whose accounts are NOT
  required to be audited.
- 31 October of the assessment year — taxpayers whose accounts are required to be
  audited (e.g. companies, and others subject to tax audit).
- 30 November of the assessment year — taxpayers required to furnish a transfer-
  pricing report (Section 92E).

A belated return can be filed under Section 139(4), and a revised return under
Section 139(5), up to 31 December of the assessment year (or before completion of
assessment, whichever is earlier). An updated return (ITR-U) under Section 139(8A)
has its own extended window.

Late-filing fee under Section 234F:
- Up to Rs. 5,000 if the return is filed after the due date.
- Restricted to Rs. 1,000 where total income does not exceed Rs. 5 lakh.
Interest under Sections 234A/234B/234C may also apply on any unpaid tax.

The Department sometimes extends these dates by notification — always verify the
current due date for the relevant assessment year at the source URL.
