# Presumptive taxation for small business and professionals (Income-tax Act, Sections 44AD, 44ADA, 44AE)

Section 44AD — eligible resident business (not profession), individual / HUF /
partnership firm (not LLP), with turnover up to the prescribed limit:
- Profit is presumed at 8% of turnover/gross receipts.
- The presumed rate is 6% for the part of turnover received through banking channels
  (account-payee cheque/draft, ECS, or other prescribed electronic modes).
- Turnover ceiling is Rs. 2 crore; it is raised to Rs. 3 crore where cash receipts
  do not exceed 5% of total turnover.
- If a person opts out of 44AD after opting in, they are barred from claiming it for
  the next five assessment years.

Section 44ADA — eligible resident professionals (e.g. legal, medical, engineering,
architecture, accountancy, technical consultancy, interior decoration):
- Profit is presumed at 50% of gross receipts.
- Gross-receipts ceiling is Rs. 50 lakh; raised to Rs. 75 lakh where cash receipts
  do not exceed 5% of gross receipts.

Section 44AE — presumptive scheme for the business of plying, hiring or leasing
goods carriages (per-vehicle presumed income).

Under presumptive schemes the assessee is generally not required to maintain regular
books of account for that business. Verify the current limits and rates against the
official Income-tax Act text and the relevant Finance Act at the source URL.
