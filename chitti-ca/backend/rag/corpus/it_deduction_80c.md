# Deduction under Section 80C (Income-tax Act, 1961)

Section 80C allows an individual or HUF a deduction from gross total income for
specified investments and payments, up to an aggregate ceiling of Rs. 1,50,000 in a
financial year (the combined 80C + 80CCC + 80CCD(1) limit).

Common eligible items include:
- Employee Provident Fund (EPF) and Public Provident Fund (PPF) contributions.
- Life insurance premium (within prescribed limits relative to sum assured).
- Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) units.
- Principal repayment of a housing loan; stamp duty and registration on a house.
- 5-year tax-saving bank fixed deposit; National Savings Certificate (NSC).
- Sukanya Samriddhi Account; tuition fees for up to two children.

Important: deductions under Chapter VI-A such as 80C are available under the OLD tax
regime. They are generally NOT available under the default NEW regime
(Section 115BAC), which instead offers a higher standard deduction and revised slabs.

Section 80CCD(1B) gives an ADDITIONAL deduction up to Rs. 50,000 for NPS, over and
above the Rs. 1.5 lakh 80C ceiling.

Verify current eligibility and limits against the official Income-tax Act text and
the latest Finance Act at the source URL.
