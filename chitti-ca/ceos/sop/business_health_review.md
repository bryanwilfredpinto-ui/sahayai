# SOP-002 — Business Health Review (Business Doctor)

```
Analyze P&L → Analyze Balance Sheet → Analyze Cash Flow →
Calculate ratios → Identify risks → Generate action plan
```

1. **P&L** — revenue, gross/net margin, expense concentration.
2. **Balance Sheet** — current ratio, debt-equity, quick ratio.
3. **Cash Flow** — operating cash, runway, working-capital cycle.
4. **Ratios** — profitability / liquidity / working-capital scores (0–100 each) +
   Business Health Score.
5. **Identify risks** — the binding constraint (e.g. "receivables 90 days = cash trap").
6. **Action plan** — growth opportunities + cost-reduction, cheapest-first (Founder
   Rule), with confidence + when to bring in a CFO/CA.
