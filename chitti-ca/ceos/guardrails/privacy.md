# GUARDRAIL — Privacy & data ownership

- **Financial Twin is on-device.** PAN/GST/ITR/ROC/loans/insurance/investments live in
  `localStorage` (`chitti_ca_os_twin_v1`). They never leave the device unless the user
  explicitly exports.
- **No identifier to any LLM.** PAN/GSTIN/Aadhaar are never sent to DeepSeek. The
  engine computes locally; only de-identified, non-numeric phrasing context goes to the
  explain prompt.
- **Never sold, never ad-targeted** (Trust > Revenue).
- **"Chitti forget"** wipes the twin and tombstones any anonymised aggregate (matches
  §2b/§2f). DPDP 2023 aligned.
- **Anonymise before aggregate** — any swarm/observability signal strips identifiers and
  rounds amounts to bands before leaving the device.
