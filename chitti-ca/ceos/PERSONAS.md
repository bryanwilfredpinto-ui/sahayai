# PERSONAS — Chitti CA OS

> The four-user accessibility contract (Blind / Deaf / Mute / Illiterate) is the
> **floor under every persona below** — not a separate group. Every persona is
> also potentially blind, deaf, mute, illiterate, elderly or rural.

## Individual

| Persona | Core need | What Chitti does first |
|---|---|---|
| **Salaried employee** | "Old vs new regime? Which deductions? Did I overpay TDS?" | Regime comparison + deduction finder + refund estimate |
| **Pensioner / senior** | "Is my pension taxable? 15H? Senior exemptions?" | Slow-speech, large-text, exemption checklist |
| **Student** | "Scholarship taxable? First ITR? Education loan 80E?" | First-ITR walkthrough, 80E explainer |
| **Homemaker** | "Gift from husband taxable? Joint account? Small savings?" | Clubbing rules in plain language |
| **NRI** | "DTAA? NRO/NRE? Property sale TDS 195?" | Residential-status test + DTAA pointers |
| **Gig worker / freelancer** | "44ADA presumptive? Advance tax? GST threshold?" | Presumptive vs books decision + advance-tax calendar |

## Business

## Professional (force-multiplier users)

CA · CS · CMA · Accountant · Tax Consultant — use Chitti as a **second brain**: a
fast checklist/second-opinion/devil's-advocate, never a replacement for their
judgement or their signature.

## Agriculture

Farmer · Dairy owner · Food processor — "Is agricultural income taxable? Kisan
schemes? FPO/Co-op benefits? Food-processing PMFME subsidy?"

## Accessibility (the floor — every persona inherits this)

| User | What is non-negotiable |
|---|---|
| **Blind** | Voice-first. Chitti reads notices, tax reports, scheme eligibility aloud. Every number spoken. |
| **Deaf** | Visual cards + ISL panel. Never audio-only. Symbol **and** word for every status. |
| **Mute** | Tap-based workflow. Voice input optional, never required. |
| **Illiterate** | Voice + icons. Picture menus. No required reading. Auto-read first result. |
| **Senior citizen** | Large text, slow speech, simple language, repeat button. |
