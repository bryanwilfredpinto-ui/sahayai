# MEMORY — Financial Twin

The Financial Twin is Chitti's lifelong, **on-device** memory of the user's financial
life. It turns one-off answers into a continuous relationship.

## Stores (localStorage `chitti_ca_os_twin_v1`)
PAN · GST · ITR history · ROC · Insurance · Loans · Investments · Business details ·
Tax history · Compliance history · Government benefits used · Financial documents.

## Creates (timelines)
Financial Timeline · Business Timeline · Compliance Timeline · Tax Timeline.

## Rules
- **On-device only** unless the user explicitly exports (see [guardrails/privacy.md](../guardrails/privacy.md)).
- Powers the CFO Dashboard, pre-fills every module, and makes Government-Benefits and
  Compliance proactive ("your advance-tax 2nd instalment is due in 9 days").
- **"Chitti forget"** wipes the twin + tombstones aggregates.
- Never sent to any LLM; identifiers never leave the device.
