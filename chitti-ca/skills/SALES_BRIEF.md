# Chitti CA — SALES_BRIEF

Plain-English pitch. No jargon, no roadmap promises. Honest stub today; useful today.

## Who this is for

Indian MSME owners, freelancers, gig workers, and salaried first-time filers who cannot afford a CA on retainer for one-line questions. Voice-in, voice-out, 12 Indian languages, free, no sign-up. See [../CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) for the four-user accessibility contract.

## Ten pain points

1. The CA bills Rs 2,000–Rs 5,000 for a five-minute "which ITR form do I file?" answer.
2. A salaried user opens Form 16 and cannot read half the cells (`Section 17(1)`, `Section 17(2)`, `Section 10(13A)`).
3. A freelancer earning Rs 8 L doesn't know `44ADA` exists.
4. A notice under `Section 143(1)` lands in the letterbox and the user panics.
5. Google searches return blogspam from 2017 quoting the wrong slab rates.
6. GST thresholds change between services and goods, between states and special-category states — confusing on day one.
7. The income-tax portal speaks legal English; a Tamil-speaking salaried mother in Coimbatore needs Tamil.
8. The CA's office is closed at 9pm; the panic is at 9pm.
9. A blind user on Android cannot navigate the income-tax portal at all.
10. Filers waste Rs 1,000 in late-fee penalties because nobody explained `234F` to them in plain Hindi.

## Ten benefits

1. Free. No sign-up. No PAN required. No card.
2. Voice-in (mic button). Voice-out (read aloud). Works for blind users.
3. Twelve Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Urdu.
4. Topic chips (ITR / GST / TDS / 80C-80D / 44AD-44ADA / Notice received) — illiterate-friendly, no typing required.
5. Plain English first, jargon second. Every section number is defined in the same sentence.
6. Reads a tax notice that the user pastes in, in their own language.
7. Never invents a section number or rate — flags "I am not certain" honestly.
8. Server-enforced disclaimer on every reply — the user is always pointed at a real CA before anything binding.
9. Stateless. No data stored. PAN / Aadhaar never echoed back.
10. Five minutes from question to "I now know what to ask my CA" — that is the unit of value.

## What we tell users honestly

This is a triage tool. It does not file. It does not look at your books. It is not your CA. It exists to make your CA appointment shorter and your money safer. See [VALUES.md](VALUES.md) and [BOUNDARIES.md](BOUNDARIES.md).
