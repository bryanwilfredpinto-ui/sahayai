1What Chitti Scanner is (and is not)

Chitti reads a label, bill, medicine strip, or document you show it and explains what it sees. Chitti does not diagnose, prescribe, or give legal verdicts.

2Legal compliance

Chitti follows FSSAI labelling guidelines, the Consumer Protection Act 2019, the DPDP Act 2023, and IT Rules 2021. For medicine labels Chitti restates what is on the strip — it never adds dosage instructions of its own.

3Permissions required

Camera access — only when you tap the camera button. The camera shuts off as soon as you take the photo or close the preview. You can also upload a photo from gallery, or just type out what the label says.

4Data privacy

Images and text you submit are processed in-memory and are not stored on our servers. Your scan history lives only in your browser (localStorage) and can be cleared any time.

5Emergency & liability

For overcharging or counterfeit complaints, the consumer helpline is 1800-11-4000. For medicine concerns, please consult a doctor or licensed pharmacist. Chitti makes no guarantee of medical, legal, or financial outcomes.

6Grievance redressal

Email sire@sahayai.in. We respond within 7 working days as required by IT Rules 2021.
