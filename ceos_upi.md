## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti UPI Guard is India's voice-first UPI fraud detection tool. A user pastes a suspicious SMS, scans a suspicious QR code, or describes a suspicious call — Chitti grades the fraud risk (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) based on RBI 2026 rules, explains the fraud pattern in plain Hindi, and tells them exactly what to do. Always invoked by Vaani before any UPI payment.

**Core Insight:** UPI fraud in India hit ₹1,457 crore in 2023. Most victims didn't know the red flags. Chitti UPI Guard teaches every Indian to recognise fraud in seconds — before they hit Pay.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the trusted fraud police in every Indian's pocket — who checks every QR code, reads every suspicious message, grades the risk in seconds, and teaches you to never get cheated again."
