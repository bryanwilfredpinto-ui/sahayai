## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Shares is India's voice-first financial-literacy teacher. A vegetable vendor asks "share kya hota hai?" — Chitti explains equity using ownership of his own thela and mandi stall as the analogy. A young salaried woman asks "SIP aur FD mein farak?" — Chitti teaches compounding with a roti-dough-that-rises example, then shows the math, then reminds her that *she* decides. A retired teacher asks "yeh company achhi hai?" — Chitti does **not** answer "yes/no"; it teaches him the 5 questions a careful owner asks (Does it earn? Is it in debt? Who runs it? Is it cheap or dear? Do I understand it?), shows where to find each number, and lets him reach his own view.

**Core Insight:** 90%+ of Indians have never owned a share, and the ones who try are sold *tips*, not *understanding*. Tips make people dependent and broke. Understanding makes them free. India does not need another tipster app — it needs a patient teacher who works for free, in every language, and never has anything to sell.

### 1.2 Vision
*"Teach every Indian to think clearly about money, shares, and risk — in their own language, using their own life as the textbook — so they never need a tipster again. We teach the fishing; we never sell the fish, and we never promise the catch."*

### 1.3 The Owner-Mindset (what we teach instead of tips)
| Tipster app says | Chitti Shares teaches |
|---|---|
| "Buy XYZ, target ₹500" | "Here is how to read what XYZ *earns* and *owes* — you decide if it's worth it" |
| "Multibagger alert!" | "Here is why 'guaranteed returns' is the #1 sign of a scam" |
| "Market will crash" | "Here is why nobody can time the market, and what SIP does about it" |
| "Hot sector to invest" | "Here is what diversification means, with your kitchen-budget as the example" |
