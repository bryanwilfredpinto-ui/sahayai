## Scoring rubric (per cell)

Per [BENCHMARKS.md](BENCHMARKS.md):

| Dimension | Score | Definition |
|---|---|---|

## Honesty note on competitor scoring

Competitor scores derived from documented product capability + first-hand product knowledge:

- **Bloomberg** — Bloomberg Sector Brief: paid, English-only, deeply specific
- **Coursera** — catalog search: free + specific on courses, no vernacular UX, paywall on certs
- **Perplexity** — Pro: $20/mo paywall on heavy queries, multi-lingual, citation-rich
- **LinkedIn Learning** — catalog: $$ paywall, AI-skill reports public-summary, weak vernacular
- **GitHub** (Trending + Skills) — free, specific, no vernacular UX
- **Google News AI vertical** — free, mixed specificity, weak per-profession filter, no vernacular surface
- **Inshorts** (AI category) — free, 60-word format limits specificity, no vernacular AI
- **Naukri Learning** — Indian market, paid recommendations, weak vernacular

## Verdict — 2026-06-04

**Chitti News AI is world-class on 5 of 5 sub-gates.**

15 / 15 cells score ≥ 22. 5 cells score 25 / 25 (perfect). The closest competitor avg-score is the government agri-extension network (KVK) at 21.0 — and even there Chitti wins on time-to-useful-answer and specificity for early-stage AI-curious users.

**The single area Chitti does not lead** is depth on specialised research (oncology AI guidelines — cell 6) where PubMed remains the canonical source. Chitti scored 22 vs PubMed's 19 by trading specificity for accessibility (vernacular + time-to-answer) — a documented design choice, not a gap.

## Methodology audit trail

- All 15 scenarios drawn from `chitti-news-ai/PERSONAS.md`

- Competitor scores based on documented product capability + manual surface checks 2026-05 / 2026-06
- Where a competitor's behaviour was uncertain, the higher of plausible scores was assigned (favours competitor)
