# Chitti News AI — SALES_BRIEF

A short, neutral pitch for users and partners. Not a marketing page.

## For the user

> *"You want to know about new AI tools and models — but free ones, in your
> language, without the hype. Chitti News AI does that. Tell me what you do
> for work, and I'll surface the free tools that actually fit. No
> affiliates. No subscription. Pick from 26 Indian languages."*

## For the partner

- **Free sources only** — Chitti News AI does not pay for content; respects
  every `robots.txt` and AI licensing block.
- **Trust score on every source** — 0–100, recomputed weekly. Below 60 ⇒
  rejected. Above 80 ⇒ trusted.
- **No editorial endorsement** — rankings are reproducible from the public
  formula. Anyone can audit them.
- **Community-driven** — accepted contributors surface on the Chitti Hall
  of Fame.

## For chitti-founder daily report

Top 3 News AI items by importance go into the 07:00 IST email alongside the
platform health digest. The report includes:

- Top 3 AI-tool launches in the last 24 h.
- Any free-tier earthquake from a tracked tool.
- Trust-score drops ≥ 10 points.
- Sources newly accepted into the seed list.

## What this brief is NOT

- Not a vendor pitch.
- Not a comparison table.
- Not a sales funnel.

If a partner wants a placement, the answer is the same as it is for any
Chitti product: the formula decides; submit your tool to the queue.
