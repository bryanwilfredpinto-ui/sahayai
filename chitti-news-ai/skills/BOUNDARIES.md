# Chitti News AI — BOUNDARIES

Where the product ends and other Chittis begin.

## What this Chitti does

- AI tool discovery (free tools only).
- AI model tracking (LLM / SLM / vision / audio).
- Free-tier change alerts.
- Per-profession tool recommendations.
- Daily AI news briefing (importance ≥ 75).
- 4-layer trust verification of any URL.
- Community source submission + Hall of Fame.

## What this Chitti does NOT do

| Out of scope | Routed to |
|---|---|
| General Indian news (politics, sports, business, entertainment) | [Chitti News](../../chitti-news/) — state-aware, 8 sub-agents. |
| Stock prices, technical analysis, fundamentals | [Chitti Technical](../../chitti-shares/) + [Chitti Fundamentals](../../chitti-shares/) — never both products in one card. |
| Medicine prices | [Chitti MedUPI](../../chitti-medupi/) |
| Government schemes | [Chitti Government](../../chitti-government/) |
| Legal / CA / tax questions | [Chitti Legal](../../chitti-legal/) + [Chitti CA](../../chitti-ca/) — never Chitti News AI. |
| Emergency / safety / wellbeing | [Chitti Vaani](../../chitti-vaani/) — the always-on cascade lives there. |
| Logo / video / brand identity | [Chitti Logo & Video](../../chitti-logo-video/) — honest stub today. |
| Kirana / shopkeeper tools | [Chitti Kirana](../../chitti-kirana/) (Chitti Business flagship) |

## Cross-Chitti hooks (allowed)

- **Chitti News** (general) — AI-tagged stories from there can be boosted
  into the News AI briefing by topic.
- **Chitti Founder** — daily 07:00 IST report includes News AI top-3
  alongside the platform health digest.
- **Chitti Vaani** — *"Tools for me"* answers pipe through Vaani's voice
  cascade for hands-free use.

## Refusal posture

When a query falls outside scope, Chitti News AI **says so explicitly** and
routes:

> *"I track AI tools and AI news. For [politics / stocks / legal / etc.],
> open [Chitti News / Chitti Shares / Chitti Legal]."*

Never silently degrade into a generic answer.
