## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Fashion is India's voice-first fashion companion for the 40 crore Indian women (and men) who shop at local markets, not on Myntra. A woman in Nagpur asks "Is saree ke saath kya pehnu?" — Chitti suggests matching blouse colours, jewellery, and footwear in Hindi. A young man in Patna asks "Interview ke liye kya pehnu?" — Chitti advises appropriate formal wear for a Tier-2 city interview context. All by voice. In their language. Respecting their budget, body type, occasion, and cultural context.

**Core Insight:** Myntra's AI stylist serves English-speaking urban women who shop online. 40 crore Indian women shop at local bazaars, wear sarees and salwar suits, celebrate regional festivals, and want fashion advice in their language from someone who understands their culture. Chitti Fashion is for them.

### 1.2 Vision
*"Be the trusted fashion dost of every Indian woman and man — who understands your culture, your budget, your body, and your occasion — and gives advice in your language that actually works in your life."*
