> *"The world's most trusted information operating system. Free. Vernacular. Auditable. Forever."*

This is the constitution of the Chitti News Operating System (CNOS). It sits above every spec, every skill, and every line of code. When any document, ticket, or instinct conflicts with this constitution, **the constitution wins.**

## Article 2 — The CNOS mission

CNOS exists so that every Indian reader, every day, in their own language and state, can answer not *"What happened?"* but *"What should I know? What should I do? What does this mean for me?"* — for free, in the vernacular, auditably, forever.

CNOS is **News + Verification + Context + Impact + Action Plan** — the five things every story should answer that no Indian aggregator does.

---

## Article 3 — The "Never Do" list

CNOS will never:

- **Lock content behind a paywall.** Free, forever, no exception.
- **Auto-play video or audio.** The reader chooses to play; CNOS never starts sound unbidden.
- **Re-write a headline.** Publisher headlines are verbatim. We link, we never editorialize.
- **Track readers off-device.** For You, Read Later, and Cancelled live in `localStorage` only — never on a backend.
- **Promote the unverified.** No story reaches the home or For You surface without passing the 5-question test; no verdict ships without ≥2-source corroboration.
- **Label a story partisan.** Politics coverage is neutral; equal coverage across parties is a hard rule.
- **Hide reading time.** Every card shows its minutes.
- **Show "Verified" without showing what verified it.** Every badge is auditable in ≤1 tap.
- **Translate-and-ship** without a real vernacular publisher's source. Bengali is not Hindi-with-Latin-script.
- **Carry ads, advertorials, or paid placement.** Ever.

---

## Article 4 — The Golden Rule (confirm before every side-effecting action)

Per the locked Chitti Golden Rule, CNOS **never acts on its own.** Every side-effecting action — saving, dismissing/cancelling, submitting feedback, sharing, asking an LLM to act — gates on an explicit confirmation. Chitti asks *"Sire, shall I do X?"* and waits for an explicit yes (voice **or** tap). It never defaults to Yes, never times out into Yes, and silence means wait — forever. Reading and listening are not side-effecting and need no confirmation; anything that changes state or leaves the device does.

---

## Article 5 — Accessibility is constitutional, not optional

The four-user contract (Blind / Deaf / Mute / Illiterate) is binding on every card and every page. No CNOS feature is "done" until it passes all four user journeys in `accessibility/`. CNOS inherits the substrate — `chitti_a11y.js`, `feedback-widget.js`, `chitti_isl.js` — and may never ship a surface that bypasses it.
