# CNOS — VISION

> *"My job is to give you the news that matters to you, in your language, in your state, in your time — without selling you outrage."*

A standalone vision for the Chitti News Operating System. Distilled from `PRODUCT_VISION.md`; this file is the crisp, quotable north.

---

## The vision in one line

CNOS is **News + Verification + Context + Impact + Action Plan** — the five things every story should answer that no Indian aggregator does — free, vernacular, auditable, forever.

---

## The 5 questions every article answers

| # | Question | How CNOS answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What happened?** | Publisher's verbatim headline + body — never re-written |
| 2 | **Why does it matter?** | Context Agent supplies the 1-line stake |
| 3 | **Who is affected?** | Personalization Agent surfaces if you — by state / language / profession — are in the affected set |
| 4 | **What happens next?** | Action Agent's 1-sentence "what to watch for" |
| 5 | **Should I care?** | The reader's own 👍/👎 + saved/cancelled state drive the For You ranking |

A story that cannot answer all five does not reach the home or For You surface. It still appears in latest-by-source — but the prime real estate is reserved for stories that pass all 5.

## The 5 strategic moats

1. **Multi-language NATIVE (not translated).** Cloudscraper-fallback ingest for Cloudflare-protected regional publishers (Saamana, Prajavani, Rozana Spokesman) is a 12-month engineering investment that translation-shops cannot shortcut.
2. **State-aware routing.** State-first ordering with a `coverage` payload that narrates gaps honestly. No US aggregator does this.
3. **Trust Strip on every card.** Verified · ≥2-source corroboration · publisher trust · reading time — in <2 s.
4. **7-agent swarm.** News → Verification → Context → Personalization → Accessibility → Career → Action — per story, deterministic where possible, explainable per agent.
5. **Per-device For You.** The personalization algorithm runs in the browser. No tracking. A structural moat against Google/Meta data-extraction aggregators.
