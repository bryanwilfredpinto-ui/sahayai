## 1. What is Chitti Business?

Chitti Business is **not** accounting software, **not** a chatbot, **not** an ERP.

> "Chitti Business = Autopilot for Small Businesses. Anything that repeats in a shop — Chitti automates it."

### 1.1 Five roles in one

| Role | Means | Example | Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💰 **CFO** | Billing, GST, profit, udhaar, cash flow | Auto GST bill → WhatsApp to customer | Shopkeeper |
| 📦 **Operations Manager** | Inventory, expiry, reorder, suppliers | Bread stock low → supplier WA sent | Shopkeeper + Supplier |
| 💬 **Customer Service** | 24/7 chatbot, orders, availability | "Milk hai?" → instant reply | Customer |
| 📊 **Growth Advisor** | Demand prediction, benchmarking | "Saturday samosa demand high — stock more" | Shopkeeper |

## 2. Proactive Learning Engine — Chitti's most powerful feature

> **Chitti does not wait for instructions. Chitti learns the routine, detects patterns, and acts automatically.** "Chitti should not wait. Chitti should anticipate."

### 2.1 Onboarding — learning the shop's DNA
Conversational, not a form.
- **Layer 1 — Basics:** open time, sales noting, stock checking, supplier rhythm, UPI/cash mix
- **Layer 2 — Pain points:** stock runs out, payment tracking, expiry issues
- **Layer 3 — Hidden insights:** expiry loss, weekend spikes, credit customers

### 2.2 Routine → task engine (automated)

| Time | Task | Type | Configurable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 AM | Morning brief — pending tasks, low stock, expiry | Auto | Yes |
| 9 AM | Shop open reminder + supplier schedule | Auto | Yes |
| 1 PM | Mid-day stock nudge | Auto | Yes |
| 5 PM | Sales summary request | Auto | Yes |
| After each bill | Feedback request to customer | Triggered | Always on |
| 9 PM | Daily night report | Auto | Yes |
| Sunday night | Weekly + next-week demand forecast | Auto | Yes |

### 2.3 Learning loop
Chitti observes → learns → adapts within weeks. Example: master ignores 1 PM check → shifted to 3 PM in week 1. Samosa demand 3× on Saturday → Friday reminder by week 3. Customer X always orders milk+bread → bundle offer suggested by month 2.

## 3. Complete inventory system

### 3.1 Per-product tracking
Product name · Stock qty · Min stock level · **Cost price** · Selling price · **Expiry date** · Supplier · Last restocked · Demand pattern.

### 3.2 Barcode scanning — WhatsApp camera
Photo of barcode → name + expiry + GST rate + supplier auto-populated.

### 3.3 Expiry management

| Days to expiry | Action | Who | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | First alert + plan | Master | "3 medicine batches expire — plan action" |
| 15 | Auto-discount scheme suggestion | Master + customers (on approval) | "10% off to clear stock?" |
| 7 | Urgent — scheme or return | Master | "Return to supplier or flash offer?" |
| 3 | Last-chance + message regular buyers | Master + loyal customers | "Aaj special offer on your regular item" |
| 0 | Remove + log waste | Master daily loss report | "Today expiry loss ₹X" |

### 3.4 Demand prediction
Saturday spike · Rain forecast → Maggi/tea/biscuits · Festival weeks · End-of-month dip · Regular customer silence (7 days) · Sunday poultry 4× · Exam season stationery · 30-day dead stock.

### 3.5 Out-of-stock customer memory
Customer asks for Limca → not available → Chitti notes name + product + date → when restocked, Chitti auto-messages: "Aapne Limca maanga tha — aaj stock aa gaya hai." **No customer is lost.**

## 4. Billing & Finance engine

### 4.1 GST-compliant bill generation
1. Item entry (text, voice, barcode)
2. GST calc — 0/5/12/18/28%
3. Professional invoice (shop name, GSTIN, customer, items, breakup, total)
4. WhatsApp delivery instantly
5. Payment follow-up if unpaid
6. Ledger update (Paid/Pending)
7. GSTR-1-ready monthly compile

### 4.2 Udhaar (credit) tracking
Per-customer ledger · Daily morning summary · Polite reminders (with permission) · Monthly report · **Fraud detection** (sales low but udhaar suspiciously high).

### 4.3 Cash flow guardian
- "Kal supplier payment ₹X. Cash low. Collect ₹5,000 from 3 customers today?"
- UPI-vs-sales mismatch flag
- Margin shrinkage alerts ("Maggi ₹1/pack — push Yippee ₹3/pack")
- Dead-stock cash blockage
- End-of-month cash projection

### 4.4 Daily Night Report (9 PM)
Total Sales · GST Collected · Pending · Top Item · Feedback Rating · Low Stock · Expiry · One suggestion for tomorrow.

### 4.5 GST filing assistance
Monthly GSTR-1 compile · Slab summary · Deadline reminder 5 days before · Pre-filled data for verification · Discrepancy explained simply.

## 16. Chitti Kirana — flagship Chitti Business product

The first concrete instantiation of Chitti Business is **Chitti Kirana** — the kirana/grocery digital employee. Full capability surface lives in `chitti-kirana/skills/`. Summary below.

### 16.1 Billing — three input modes
- **Voice billing** — Master speaks items ("2 kg atta, 1 Maggi, 6 eggs") → Chitti generates bill
- **Camera billing** — barcode scan via WhatsApp camera → bill auto-built
- **Video billing** — Master shows shelf on video → Chitti reads visible items
- Bill delivered via **WhatsApp / SMS link** to the customer

### 16.2 Bill-link flywheel — every bill is an acquisition channel
When the customer opens the bill link:
1. Chitti greets by **name in customer's language**
2. Asks for feedback (single tap or voice note)
3. **Upsell:** "Next time order on WhatsApp — bhej dijiye, hum prepare kar denge"
4. **Offer Chitti PA:** "I can be your personal assistant too — FREE"
5. New PA user acquired through the kirana flywheel

### 16.3 Language strategy
- **Auto-detect customer language** (no language picker shoved in their face)
- Speak **80% in their language**, 20% Hindi as filler ("bhaiya / didi / ji")
- Address by **name with respect** always
- Never force English on a kirana customer
- Falls back through the [Chitti Voice Factory](CHITTI_VOICE_FACTORY_MASTER_SPEC.md) 4-supplier cascade

### 16.4 Throughput — honest under load
- **Queue management** for peak load (evening rush, festival days)
- **Honest wait-time message** when queued — never silent
- **Target: <20s** end-to-end response for billing
- **DeepSeek fallback queue** built in — if primary slot is full, the request is queued not dropped, and the customer is told the wait
