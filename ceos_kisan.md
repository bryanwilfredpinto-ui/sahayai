## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary

Chitti Kisan is India's voice-first agriculture and gardening companion — for everyone from a farmer with 5 acres in Vidarbha to a homemaker growing tomatoes on her Mumbai balcony.

It tells the Vidarbha farmer when to water his soybean (real-time soil moisture + IMD weather), what organic alternative replaces urea (ICAR database), today's soybean price at the nearest mandi (Agmarknet API), which government scheme he missed (Chitti Government integration), and whether his cow's symptoms need a vet (Pashu Helpline 1962 — always).

It tells the Mumbai homemaker exactly which vegetables to grow in her 6x4 balcony, how much money she'll save monthly, and connects her to a local farmer for fresh vegetables directly.

And it connects farmers directly to consumers — no middlemen — through a community marketplace where Agmarknet-based fair prices are transparent to both sides.

**Core Insight:** India has 14 crore farmers + 8 crore urban gardeners = 22 crore potential users. Not one app serves both with voice, in their language, with real-time data, organic focus, and fair market access. Chitti Kisan does all of this — free.

### 1.2 Vision
*"From 5-acre wheat fields to 6x4 balcony gardens — every Indian who grows something deserves a trusted companion who knows their soil, reads the weather, finds the fair price, guides their animals, and connects them directly to consumers who want fresh food."*

## SECTION 23: SOP (6 PROCEDURES)

| SOP | Name | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| SOP 01 | Crop Advice | User location (GPS) → NBSS&LUP soil type → IMD weather → crop calendar → "Aaj paani doon?" → yes/no + reason → "Kaunsa pesticide?" → organic first → chemical only if organic fails |
| SOP 02 | Mandi Price | "Aaj gehun ka bhav?" → Agmarknet API → nearest 3 mandis + price → "Best mandi: [X] at ₹[Y]/quintal" → "Ye price sahi hai ya nahi?" → 7-day trend |
| SOP 03 | Livestock Daily | User selects animal type → daily checklist by voice → "Aaj milk kitna tha?" → log → trend → vaccination due? → "Lakshan kya hain?" → if ANY disease symptoms → IMMEDIATE 1962 |
| SOP 04 | Urban Garden | "Mere balcony mein kya ugaun?" → ask space (sq ft) → suggest "Grow Your Own Thali" → pot sizes → soil mix → watering schedule → "Month 1 mein yeh dikhega" → progress tracking |
| SOP 05 | Farm-to-Table | Farmer: "Mere paas 50kg tamatar hai" → register product → FSSAI check → list at Agmarknet fair price → Consumer: "Paas mein organic tamatar?" → match → WhatsApp connection → direct deal |
| SOP 06 | Chitti-to-Chitti B2B | Kirana Chitti: "Kal 10kg tamatar chahiye" → Chitti Kisan receives request → matches nearest farmer with surplus → aggregates demand if needed → connects farmer and Kirana → tracks order |
