# PRODUCT_VISION — Chitti Universal Scanner

## One-line

**The user scans. Chitti detects, routes, explains, and remembers — so nobody ever has to
know which Chitti to open.**

## The problem

Every app forces a decision the most vulnerable users cannot make:
*"which app do I open for this?"* An illiterate farmer, a blind grandmother, a
first-time-smartphone delivery rider — they cannot navigate a 13-product menu. They *can*
hold a phone in front of a thing and tap one button. Today they get nothing.

## The product

A single capture surface (camera · gallery · type · voice) that:

1. **Detects** the category of whatever was scanned (deterministic rules first; vision LLM
   as enhancement when funded).
2. **Routes** it to the right specialist Chitti — automatically, with a confidence score.
3. **Explains** *why* it routed there (no black box; tap 🤖 Chitti).
4. **Hands off** to the specialist (deep-link / Vaani intent), or says **COMING SOON**
   honestly when that specialist isn't built yet.
5. **Remembers** the scan as a life event (Universal Memory / Life Twin), local-first.

## What makes it different

| Ordinary scanner app | Chitti Universal Scanner |
|---|---|
| OCR's text and stops | Detects category → routes to a specialist |
| Black-box result | Explains *why* it decided (Trust Layer) |
| One vertical (e.g. food only) | 13 verticals behind one button |
| Needs internet + a vision model | Deterministic core works offline; LLM only enhances |
| Forgets every scan | Every scan is a remembered, searchable life event |
| Ignores disabled users | Blind/Deaf/Mute/Illiterate are the design floor |
| Sells your data | User-owned, anonymised, "Chitti forget" deletes all |

## The moat

- **Universal routing** — nobody else turns "point at anything" into "the right specialist
  answers." This is the OS-level abstraction (CEOS).
- **Family Knowledge Graph** (Level 8) — Father · Mother · Child · Vehicle · Home · Farm ·
  Medicine · Insurance · Certificates, all linked. *"Show everything expiring in 30 days."*
  Nobody has this for Bharat families. (Built honestly: local-first today; cross-device
  graph is **COMING SOON** until backend persistence is verified — see the RED note in
  [CEOS_ARCHITECTURE.md](CEOS_ARCHITECTURE.md).)
- **Accessibility-first** — the gap (blind+deaf+mute+illiterate+rural+budget) is the reason
  to exist.

— I'm sending it to MedUPI to find a cheaper Jan Aushadhi option,"* and gets the answer read
