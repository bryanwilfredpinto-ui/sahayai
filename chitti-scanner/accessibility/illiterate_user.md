# accessibility/illiterate_user.md — Level 9

> Illiterate = **icon / voice-first.** No reading required, anywhere.

## Requirements

- **Picture menus** — category pick is emoji/icon tiles (💊 medicine · 🍪 food · 🏍️ vehicle ·
  👕 clothing · 📄 document · 🌿 crop · 📷 not sure), each spoken on tap.
- **Voice-everything** — the route, the reason, the result, the recall, all spoken in the
  user's language.
- **Plain language** — class-5 Hindi/regional; no jargon, no English fine print read silently.
- **One step at a time** — never an overwhelming wall of options (also serves cognitive
  profile).
- **The capture itself is the input** — point the phone, tap once; typing/reading optional.

This is the persona the Universal Scanner exists for first: *the user who cannot choose a
product because they cannot read the product names.* The Scanner removes the reading step.
