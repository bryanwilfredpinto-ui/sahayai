# PERSONAS — Chitti Universal Scanner · Level 3

> The Universal Scanner is built **first** for the users who cannot choose a product.
> Every persona below must complete *scan → route → understand* without knowing a single
> Chitti name.

## P1 — Lakshmi, 68, illiterate widow, Tier-3 town (PRIMARY)

- **Context:** Cannot read. Holds her late husband's medicine strips and a "tax notice".
- **Need:** "What is this? Is it important? What do I do?"
- **Scanner job:** Detect medicine → route to MedUPI (Jan Aushadhi alternative, read aloud).
  Detect tax notice → route to Government + Legal (explain in plain Hindi, by voice).
- **Success:** She chose no product, read nothing, and understood both.

## P2 — Ravi, 31, delivery rider, first smartphone (PRIMARY)

- **Context:** His bike is his livelihood. Dashboard light came on; got a repair quote.
- **Need:** "Is this serious? Is the ₹4,000 quote fair?"
- **Scanner job:** Detect dashboard / vehicle → route to 2-Wheeler Doctor (Scam Shield).
- **Success:** Knew whether to ride, and whether he was being overcharged.

## P3 — Meena, 45, blind, urban (PRIMARY — accessibility floor)

- **Context:** Uses a screen reader; cannot see the camera frame.
- **Need:** Scan a food packet and a UPI QR she was sent.
- **Scanner job:** Voice-guided capture; detect food → Scanner food path; detect UPI QR →
  Fraud Guard. Every step and result spoken; no visual-only content.
- **Success:** Completed both scans entirely by voice.

## P4 — Arjun, 26, deaf, ISL-first

- **Need:** Scan a clothing tag and a job-offer document.
- **Scanner job:** Caption + symbol + ISL panel on every result. Detect clothing → Fashion;
  detect resume/job-doc → Career (**COMING SOON**, honest, routes to Vaani meanwhile).
- **Success:** Never needed audio; saw where Chitti routed and why.

## P5 — Sunita, 38, farmer, rural low-connectivity

- **Need:** Scan a diseased crop leaf and a pesticide bottle label.
- **Scanner job:** Detect crop/leaf → Farmer (**COMING SOON**, honest); detect pesticide
  label → Scanner generic + safety warning. 2G-safe; deterministic, no vision spend.
- **Success:** Got honest help and a clear "this part is coming soon", never a fake answer.

## P6 — Priya, 34, daughter-caregiver (the Family Graph user)

- **Context:** Manages her parents', her kids', the car's, and the home's paperwork.
- **Need:** "Show me everything expiring in the next 30 days."
- **Scanner job:** Universal Memory remembers every scanned medicine/insurance/warranty/
  certificate; Family Graph links them (local-first now; cross-device **COMING SOON**).
- **Success:** One question surfaces every upcoming expiry across the whole family.

## P7 — Karthik, 19, student

- **Need:** Scan homework / a diagram / a certificate.
- **Scanner job:** Detect education content → Education (**COMING SOON**, honest).
- **Success:** Understood the honest roadmap, routed to the closest live help.
