# VISION — Chitti Universal Scanner · Level 2

## Mission

Create **one camera button** capable of understanding anything a human points at — and
routing it to the right specialist Chitti, automatically, in the user's language, by voice.

## The end state (Level 13)

The final product is **not** Chitti Fashion, or Chitti Mechanic, or Chitti Farmer.
**Those become modules.** The real product becomes **CHITTI OS**:

> One Camera · One Voice · One Memory · One Family Graph · One Universal Scanner ·
> Infinite Specialists.

## Why this matters for Bharat

The combination **blind + deaf + mute + illiterate + rural + first-time-smartphone** is a
genuine market gap no global vision app serves. A daughter in Delhi can scan her father's
medicine strip in Bhopal and get the Jan-Aushadhi alternative; a farmer can scan a
diseased leaf; an elderly widow can scan a "tax notice" she can't read and have it
explained in plain Hindi by voice. **The product that requires you to already know which
app to open has already failed these users.** The Universal Scanner removes that step.

## What the Vision is NOT (honest scope)

- It is **not** a diagnosis engine. Health scans escalate to "see a doctor", never name a
  disease (the safety envelope suppresses disease names — inherited from Health Scanner).
- It is **not** a magic vision model that exists today. Camera **auto-detect** of arbitrary
  objects needs a funded vision LLM; until then the deterministic text/type router carries
  the product and the camera auto-detect surfaces an honest **COMING SOON**.
- It is **not** a replacement for the specialist Chitti — it is the **front door** to it.
