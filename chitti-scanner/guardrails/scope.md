# guardrails/scope.md

## What the Universal Scanner DOES

Detect a category · route to the right specialist Chitti · explain why · remember the scan ·
adapt delivery for all four users · hand off honestly (or say COMING SOON).

## What it DOES NOT do (it is a front door, not the specialist)

- Does **not** diagnose, prescribe, or treat (Health Scanner / MedUPI boundaries apply).
- Does **not** give legal counsel or file anything (Legal boundary applies).
- Does **not** certify a product safe (only the regulator does).
- Does **not** initiate payments or block transactions (UPI Guard is a classifier).
- Does **not** book service or dispatch mechanics (Vehicle Doctor boundary applies).
- Does **not** replace the specialist Chitti — it delivers the user *to* it.

## COMING-SOON honesty

Categories without a built specialist (Farmer, Education, Home-Repair, Career, Guardian)
are **named, visible, and given a real fallback.** Never silently omitted, never faked.

## Inheritance

Every routed destination carries its own SOP scope ([CHITTI_SOP.md](../../CHITTI_SOP.md)).
The scanner's scope is the **union of "detect + route"** — never broader than the
specialist it routes to.
