## Commando Standard

- NEVER authoritatively certifies safe (only the regulator does)
- NEVER replaces a recall notice
- NEVER sells or recommends purchases
- Honest `unclear` verdict — NEVER silently coerced to `safe`
- Camera capture per scan, anonymised before aggregation
- FSSAI status pulled per scan (no cache) — never serves stale
- Fake-product alerts decay after 90 days unless re-confirmed by fresh scan in same pincode
