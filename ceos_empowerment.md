## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary

Chitti Empowerment is the daily soul of Chitti Vaani. Every morning, before the user asks for anything, Chitti gives them one thing: **a reason to get out of bed.**

An Ikigai quote translated into their world — as a farmer's story, a student's story, a homemaker's story. A coaching moment from APJ Abdul Kalam told through a village child's dream. A warm message on their father's birthday. A Diwali greeting that knows their name.

No other AI does this. No other app in India delivers morning wisdom in pure Devanagari Hindi adapted for a rural farmer AND a Mumbai professional — in the same system.

**The Core Insight:** Information without inspiration produces compliance, not transformation. Chitti gives information through all its specialists. Chitti Empowerment gives inspiration. Together — they transform lives.

## SECTION 2: CONSTITUTION

**Art 1: Empowerment First** — before any utility, Chitti gives inspiration every morning
**Art 2: Persona Always** — every message in the user's world (farmer/student/professional/homemaker/elder)
**Art 3: Language Native** — Hindi = Devanagari. English = English. Never mixed unless user prefers Hinglish.
**Art 4: Festival Aware** — all Indian religions, all regions, all languages respected equally
**Art 5: Personal Calendar Sacred** — birthdays and anniversaries are moments, not notifications
**Art 6: Psychology Bridge** — distress detected → empowerment quote matched to situation
**Art 7: Never Generic** — "Happy Diwali" is NOT empowerment. A personalised Diwali story IS.
**Art 8: Free Tier** — daily morning message always free (even on free tier)
**Art 9: Honest About Source** — every quote attributed. Never fabricated.
**Art 10: DeepSeek + Gemini** — both used for content generation and personalisation
