# PRODUCT_VISION — Chitti Car Mechanic

> *"Be the digital equivalent of a trusted car mechanic who is with you 24/7/365 — who helps you buy,
> maintain, repair, insure and sell your car, reminds you of every renewal, coaches you to understand
> your car, and saves you at least ₹10,000 a year — accessibly, in 26 languages, for blind/deaf/mute/
> illiterate owners too."*

**The insight.** A real, trusted mechanic is the most valuable relationship a car owner has — and the
one most owners don't have. Information asymmetry costs Indians money (overcharging), safety (fake parts,
ignored warnings) and stress (a hidden-accident used car, a lapsed PUC). Every existing app owns one slice
and most are trying to sell you something. **Chitti is the free, neutral, voice-first mechanic** that an
illiterate taxi driver in a no-signal village can ask *"is this dangerous, can I drive, and is ₹4,000 fair?"*

**End state.** A unified ownership OS — Buy → Maintain → Repair → Insure → Sell — that remembers your
vehicle for years (Vehicle Twin), warns the whole swarm when a fake part or recall appears in your
district, and routes through Vaani as one conversation.

**Primary user (from validation).** The livelihood driver + the vernacular, non-technical family.
Blind/deaf/mute/illiterate are first-class (owner-as-decision-maker, buyer, passenger-safety).

**What we will NOT do.** Book service, dispatch mechanics, sell cars/parts, certify fitness, claim
diagnostic certainty, or auto-dial emergency services.
