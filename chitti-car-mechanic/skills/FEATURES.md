# Chitti Car Mechanic — Features

What Chitti Car Mechanic can do for you. Free, by voice, in your language — 24 hours, 365 days.

## Live now

- **Diagnose a problem** — tell Chitti the noise, smell or warning; get the likely cause, whether it's safe to drive, and if you can fix it yourself.
- **OBD code decoder** — type a code like P0300; Chitti explains it in plain words and says if it's safe to drive.
- **Can I fix it myself?** — Chitti tells you Safe-DIY 🟢, Caution 🟡, or Mechanic-only 🔴. Safety-critical work is always a mechanic.
- **Is this quote fair? (Scam shield)** — enter the mechanic's price; Chitti flags overcharging against the expected India range.
- **Used-car buy check** — score a second-hand car, catch a critical fail (accident / odometer / loan), and get a fair offer to negotiate.
- **Sell assistant** — listing price, realistic selling price, and tips to get more.
- **Smart reminders** — never miss insurance, PUC, RC, service, timing belt, EMI; by date OR km, whichever comes first.
- **Insurance comparison** — see better-value insurers ranked by claim-settlement ratio, with indicative savings.
- **PUC check** — know if your pollution certificate is valid and the fine risk if not.
- **Which oil / which tyre / battery** — the right grade and tyre for your car and use; tread + age check; battery life.
- **Fuel & CNG/EV payback** — compare running costs and see the honest payback time before you switch.
- **My Car (Vehicle Twin)** — save your car once; Chitti remembers it for reminders and ownership scores. Stays on your phone. "Chitti forget" wipes it.
- **Savings tracker** — every rupee Chitti helps you save, toward the ₹10,000/year goal.
- **Read aloud + 26 languages** — every answer can be spoken; switch the whole app to your language.

- **Snap-the-bill** — photograph a service bill; Chitti reads it aloud and files it for you.
- **Dashboard-light camera** — snap a warning light; Chitti tells you what it means and if you can drive.

- **Reminders by WhatsApp / SMS / voice call** — delivered the way that reaches you.

## On Chitti app (Android)

- **Offline mode** — diagnose and check reminders with no internet.
- **Voice-call + SMS reminders** for low-connectivity areas.

## How Chitti keeps you safe

- Chitti never claims certainty — when unsure, it says "see a mechanic".
- Safety-critical repairs (brakes, airbag, fuel, EV high-voltage) are always "go to a mechanic".
- In an emergency, Chitti alerts your family first and never dials on its own.
- Your car data stays on your phone. Chitti never sells it.
