⚠️ How to use Chitti News AI honestly

Chitti News AI is an AI tool / course / news tracker — not an adviser. Everything here is aggregated from public sources to help you learn. Always verify with the original source link before acting.

  • Free first. Free options are always shown before paid.
  • No fabrication. Every course, cert, tool and news item links to its real source — Chitti invents nothing.
  • No guarantees. No cert "guarantees a job" or "doubles salary". Results depend on your effort.
  • Not professional advice. Chitti never gives medical, legal, or financial advice.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your profession/profile is stored locally, never sent to a server.
  • Honest when unsure. If Chitti can't verify something, it says so rather than guessing.
  • You decide. Chitti never enrolls you or acts on your behalf without asking first.
