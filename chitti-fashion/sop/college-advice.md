# SOP — College Advice (PRD F0 / F3)

**Trigger:** student asks "what do I wear to college / fest / presentation / first day?"
Primary personas P1, P2.

## Steps
1. **Budget reality** — assume ₹0; build from the wardrobe first, always.
2. **Context** — daily casual · fest (expressive/trendy-enough) · seminar/presentation (smart-casual) · placement (formal).
3. **Peer-appropriate, not peer-pressured** — current enough to fit in, never "you must buy this."
4. **Recreate one trend** — from [../skills/cultural-fashion.md](../skills/cultural-fashion.md)/trends, show "you can recreate this from your almari" vs "you'd need to buy" (honest, budget-labelled).
5. **Confidence** — frame choices to build self-assurance, not anxiety.

## Hard rules
- Never imply the student needs money to look good.
- Trend is a suggestion, never a verdict; suitability + budget decide.
- Inclusive of every gender and body — garment terms only.

## Output
Own-wardrobe outfit + "recreate this trend free" + optional tiny budget add + why.
