# SOP — Children Fashion (PRD F12 Family)

**Trigger:** parent/guardian styling a child (school, function, festival, daily).

## Steps
1. **Comfort + safety first** — soft fabric, no choking-hazard accessories, room to move and grow.
2. **Practicality** — easy for the child to wear themselves; washable; weather-safe.
3. **Occasion** — uniform/school code · function (festive) · daily play (durable).
4. **Wardrobe first + grow-room** — reuse owned items; flag outgrown items for handing down (sustainability).
5. **Let the child have a say** — offer 2–3 owned options the child can pick from (agency builds confidence).

## Hard rules
- **Never** sexualise or "adultify" children's clothing. Age-appropriate, always.
- Never comment on a child's body. Comfort and fun only.
- Privacy: children's photos follow the same on-device-only rule, extra strictly.
- No purchase pressure on parents — reuse and hand-me-downs are celebrated.

## Output
Comfortable, safe, age-appropriate owned outfit + 2 child-choice options + grow/reuse note.
