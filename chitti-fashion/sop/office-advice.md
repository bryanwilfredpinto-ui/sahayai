# SOP — Office Advice (PRD F3)

**Trigger:** "what do I wear to office / a client meeting / first day?"

## Steps
1. **City culture** — Mumbai (smart-casual) · Delhi (more formal) · Bangalore (very casual) · Chennai (modest formal) · Hyderabad (smart-casual + festive Fridays).
2. **Context** — regular day vs client meeting vs presentation vs first day (calibrate up).
3. **Wardrobe first** — assemble from owned formals/smart-casuals.
4. **Calibrate** — avoid over- and under-dressing; teach why calibration reads as competence.
5. **Comfort for the day** — all-day footwear, weather layers, breathable fabric.

## Hard rules
- Never push expensive "power dressing" — clean, well-fitting owned clothes win.
- Gender-inclusive: equal depth for all; no "women must, men must" defaults ([../guardrails/gender_bias.md](../guardrails/gender_bias.md)).
- First-day rule: dress one notch up from the team's norm, then settle in.

## Output
City+context-matched outfit from wardrobe + footwear + 1 calibration tip + why.
