# SOP — Wardrobe Audit / Closet AI (PRD F6)

**Trigger:** "audit my wardrobe" / "what should I wear more" / first time opening Almari with items.

## Steps
1. **Read** the on-device wardrobe (text triplets only).
2. **Count** per category; render stats card.
3. **Find gaps** — e.g. "6 tops, 1 bottom — one more bottom unlocks 5 more outfits."
4. **Find rare-worn** — items unworn > 6 months (or added > 6 months ago with no `last_worn`).
5. **Reuse plan** — pair each rare-worn item into a fresh owned outfit (sustainability win).
6. **Teach** — why a gap limits combinations; how one versatile piece multiplies outfits.

## Hard rules
- Gaps are framed as **opportunity to reuse**, not pressure to buy.
- Only suggest a purchase if a genuine, repeated occasion gap exists — and then budget-first.
- Never shame the size of the wardrobe ("you have so little" is forbidden).

## Output
Stats · gaps · rare-worn revival outfits · 1 optional "if you ever buy" note.
