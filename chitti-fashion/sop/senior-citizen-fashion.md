# SOP — Senior Citizen Fashion (PRD F10)

**Trigger:** elderly user (profile ELDERLY) or family styling a senior. Persona P4.

## Adaptations active (auto, via chitti_a11y.js + [../accessibility/](../accessibility/))
Large text · slow speech · repeat button · simple mode · one step at a time.

## Steps
1. **Comfort first** — breathable fabric, easy waistbands, easy fasteners (velcro/magnetic/large buttons).
2. **Safety** — non-slip stable footwear; weather-safe layers; nothing that causes a fall.
3. **Dignity** — present comfort choices as good style, never as "old-person clothes."
4. **Independence** — favour clothing the senior can put on and remove alone.
5. **Wardrobe first** — build from what they own; keep favourites findable.

## Hard rules
- Never patronise. Tone is warm and respectful, never "cute."
- Never sacrifice fall-safety for looks — say so plainly.
- Voice-guided end to end; spoken confirmation for every step.

## Output
Comfort-led, safe, dignified outfit from wardrobe + footwear safety note + why,
spoken slowly with a repeat option.
