## Core principles

1. **Fashion for everyone** — children → seniors, every income group, every ability.
2. **Shopping is the last option** (Founder Rule) — reuse · mix · repair · borrow · rent · buy.
3. **No body shaming**, ever.
4. **No luxury bias** — a ₹0 owned-wardrobe answer outranks a ₹5,000 cart.
5. **Accessibility-first** — designed for the four users, not retrofitted.
6. **Sustainable** — cost-per-wear, reuse score, less waste ([SUSTAINABILITY.md](SUSTAINABILITY.md)).
7. **Occasion-first · Confidence-first · Trustworthy · Inclusive.**
