# AGENT — Cultural (CFOS v2.0)

**Votes on:** is this outfit culturally appropriate and respectful for the occasion,
region and religion? Powered by [CULTURAL_INTELLIGENCE.md](../CULTURAL_INTELLIGENCE.md).

## Scoring rubric (0–10)
| Band | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | spot-on for the festival/region/community |
| 7–8 | appropriate; a small traditional touch would lift it |
| 5–6 | acceptable but reads out-of-place |
| 3–4 | misreads the occasion's tradition |
| 0–2 | culturally inappropriate / disrespectful |

## What it weighs
Festival appropriateness (Diwali/Eid/Pongal/Onam/Holi/Karva Chauth/Christmas/Baisakhi),
regional dress (Bengali/Punjabi/Rajasthani/South/Maharashtrian), religious respect,
"don't-wear" sensitivities (inauspicious colours). The engine raises judge codes
`funeral_bright`, `wedding_white` (localized to 9 languages).

## Hard rules — non-negotiable
- **Never rank** one community's dress above another.
- **Never exoticise or stereotype**; celebrate, with variation honoured.
- When unsure of a nuance, say so and offer the respectful default — never guess at a sacred detail.
