## Who it serves (our users)

Tier-2/3, budget-constrained, vernacular. Six goal tracks: **personal stylist · tailor/
boutique · fashion student · content creator · reseller/thrift · earn-from-home**.

## What each track returns (deterministic)

- **Skills to build** — the 3–4 concrete skills for that goal.
- **Learn** — real courses, **cost-tagged honestly**: FREE · FREE-to-learn (audit) · GOVT-FREE · LOW ₹.
- **Free / govt certificates** — real, claimable.
- **Free tools** — real (Canva, phone camera, free AI image tools).
- **First step this week** — one concrete, do-today action (the most important field).

## Honesty rules

- **Every resource is real and cost-tagged.** Never invent a course or a certificate.
- **Free first.** Paid (Udemy) is clearly tagged LOW ₹ and never the default.
- **First-step bias.** The coach always ends with one free, do-this-week action — the
  thing that actually changes a life, not a reading list.
- Localized to the 9 primary languages; resource/brand names stay English (proper nouns, §6).

## Stale-data rule

Resource list reviewed quarterly (providers change free tiers). A resource whose free
tier disappears is re-tagged or removed — never left claiming FREE dishonestly.
