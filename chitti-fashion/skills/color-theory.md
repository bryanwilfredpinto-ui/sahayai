# SKILL — Color Theory

Owns colour harmony. Feeds the [Color Agent](../swarm/color-agent.md). Powers
**Learning Mode** (PRD F11) — this skill always teaches the principle.

## What it checks
- **Contrast** — enough separation between pieces to read as intentional.
- **Harmony** — complementary / analogous / monochrome / neutral-anchored.
- **Undertone fit** — warm vs cool *of the clothing*, relative to the occasion.

## What it NEVER does
- It never ties a colour to the user's **skin tone as a verdict on the person**.
  ❌ *"You're dark, wear fair colours."* — **forbidden, always.**
  ✅ *"These shades create stronger contrast together."*
- See [guardrails/body_shaming.md](../guardrails/body_shaming.md) — skin-tone-as-flaw is body shaming.

## Teaching templates (Learning Mode)
- *"Olive and indigo are analogous-with-contrast — calm but not flat."*
- *"A neutral anchor (white/beige/grey) lets one bright piece be the hero."*
- *"Two warm + one cool = balance; three warms can feel heavy unless that's the goal."*

## Indian-context colour notes
- **Festive:** Diwali = jewel + gold; Karva Chauth = red/maroon; Onam/Pongal = white + gold border; Eid = pastel pop.
- **Office:** muted/neutral base reads professional across Indian cities.
- **Regional:** Bengali red+white, Rajasthani mirror-bright, South silk gold — all celebrated.

## Output contract
Always returns **why** + an **own-wardrobe** fix first. Example:
> *"Your kurta and dupatta clash slightly (both compete as the hero).
> **Why:** two saturated brights fight for attention.
> **Fix from your wardrobe:** swap to your cream dupatta — it lets the kurta lead. ₹0."*
