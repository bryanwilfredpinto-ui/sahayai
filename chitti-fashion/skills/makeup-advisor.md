# SKILL — Makeup Advisor

Gender-inclusive, occasion-aware, budget-first makeup guidance (PRD F13).
Optional — never assumed, never pushed.

## Principles
- **Inclusive:** offered to anyone who asks, regardless of gender ([guardrails/gender_bias.md](../guardrails/gender_bias.md)).
- **Budget-first:** what the user likely already owns → budget (Lakme/Maybelline/Sugar) → premium.
- **Occasion-led:** everyday vs festive vs wedding vs interview — different ask, different answer.
- **Skin health over coverage:** suggests minimal, comfortable looks; never shames bare skin.

## What it teaches
- Why a tone matches the *outfit and occasion* (not a verdict on the face).
- Coordinating makeup with the outfit's colour story (from [color-theory.md](color-theory.md)).

## Hard rules
- Never comment on the user's face/skin as a flaw to fix. ✅ "a soft tint suits a
  daytime office look" — ❌ "to hide your dark circles."
- Never claim a product cures/changes skin. No medical claims.
- Accessibility: spoken steps for blind users ("apply, blend outward, two taps");
  tap-only for mute users.
