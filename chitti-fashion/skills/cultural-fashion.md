# SKILL — Cultural Fashion

Owns Indian occasion, festival and regional dress knowledge. Governed by
[guardrails/cultural_sensitivity.md](../guardrails/cultural_sensitivity.md) — celebrate, never stereotype.

## Festival appropriateness
| Festival | Spirit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Diwali | bright + festive jewellery | jewel tones, gold accents |
| Eid | pastel pop, elegant | soft palettes, fine fabric |
| Pongal / Onam | traditional white + gold border | regional South Indian |
| Holi | white + okay-if-ruined | nothing precious |
| Karva Chauth | red / maroon | traditional auspicious |
| Christmas | festive reds/greens or formal | regional Goan/Mangalorean variation |
| Baisakhi | bright Punjabi festive | phulkari celebrated |

## Wedding outfit hierarchy (formality scales with closeness)
own wedding > sibling's > close friend's > colleague's. Lehengas / sarees /
sherwanis at the top; smart-Indian-formal lower down. See [../sop/wedding-advice.md](../sop/wedding-advice.md).

## Office culture by city
Mumbai (smart-casual usually) · Delhi (more formal) · Bangalore (very casual) ·
Chennai (modest formal) · Hyderabad (smart-casual + festive Fridays). See [../sop/office-advice.md](../sop/office-advice.md).

## Regional dress (celebrated, never caricatured)
- **Bengali** — red+white tant, classic and elegant.
- **Rajasthani** — mirror-work, bandhej.
- **South Indian** — silk, jasmine, temple jewellery.
- **Punjabi** — phulkari, paranda.

## Indo-western fusion — always celebrated
Kurta + jeans · saree + sneakers · dhoti pants + crop top · anarkali + boots.

## Hard rules
- Never imply one region/religion's dress is "more correct" than another.
- Respect religious dress as dignified, never exotic.
- When unsure of a regional nuance, say so honestly and offer the safe respectful default.
