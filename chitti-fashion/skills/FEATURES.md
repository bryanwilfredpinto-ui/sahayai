## LIVE — what Chitti Fashion does today

### Dress Me From What I Own
Chitti builds complete outfits using only the clothes already in your almari. ₹0.
Tap once, hear three outfits for tomorrow, each explained.

### My Almari (Wardrobe)
Add your clothes with a photo. Chitti remembers your tops, bottoms, footwear,
bags, jewellery and dupattas. Your photos stay on your phone — never uploaded.

### Outfit Review
Show or describe today's outfit. Seven Chitti experts vote — fit, colour,
occasion, comfort, confidence, budget, accessibility — and tell you what works
and one easy fix from clothes you own.

### Describe My Outfit
For blind users: Chitti tells you aloud exactly what you are wearing and whether
it suits where you are going.

### Occasion Ready
Going to a wedding, interview, office or festival? Chitti tells you if your
outfit is right — too casual, just right, or over-dressed — and fixes it.

### Weather Ready
Chitti checks your city's weather and advises the right fabric and layers.

### Budget Options
When you truly need to buy, Chitti shows Free (from your wardrobe), Budget and
Premium — and is honest about where it is cheaper.

### Wardrobe Audit
Chitti audits your almari — what you have too much or too little of, what pairs
well, and which clothes you haven't worn in months (wear them again).

### Festival & Cultural Dressing
Diwali, Eid, Pongal, Onam, Karva Chauth — and regional dress from across India,
celebrated, never stereotyped.

### Confidence Coach
For interviews and big days — Chitti helps you look polished and feel confident.
Never "you look bad" — always "this will make you shine."

### Learning Mode
Chitti doesn't just tell you — it teaches you WHY colours match, WHY a cut works,
WHY an accessory clashes. You get smarter every time.

### Accessibility Fashion
Adaptive dressing for seniors, low-vision and seated users — easy fasteners,
comfortable drape, high-contrast safe combinations.

### Outfit Simulator
Chitti instantly builds up to 30 complete outfits from the clothes you already
own — each with a confidence score — using a deterministic engine that works even
when the internet AI is down.

### Wardrobe ROI
If you must buy, Chitti tells you which single item unlocks the most new outfits
from clothes you already own — e.g. "a beige chino unlocks 18 new outfits." Spend
smart.

### Confidence Score
Every recommendation shows a confidence % and the reasons behind it — ✓ occasion,
✓ colour, ✓ weather, ✓ budget — so you can trust the advice.

### Travel Packing
Tell Chitti where you're going and for how long — it packs a mix-and-match list
from your own wardrobe.

### Emergency Fashion
Five minutes to an event? Chitti picks the fastest good outfit from clean clothes
you own.

### Become a Pro — Fashion Career Coach
Chitti can lift you into earning. Pick a goal — personal stylist, tailor/boutique,
fashion student, content creator, reseller, or earn-from-home — and Chitti gives you
real **free / low-cost** courses, **government certificates**, free tools, and one
do-this-week first step. Built for budget-first users; everything cost-tagged honestly.
