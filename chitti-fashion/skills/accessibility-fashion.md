# SKILL — Accessibility Fashion

Owns adaptive dressing — the surface almost no fashion app builds (PRD F10).
Serves seniors, low-vision, seated/wheelchair users, limited-dexterity users.
Governed by [guardrails/disability_rules.md](../guardrails/disability_rules.md).

## Principles
- **Comfort and dignity are style.** Adaptive choices are presented as good
  fashion, never as a compromise or a medical accommodation.
- **Independence first.** Favour clothing the user can put on and take off alone.

## Guidance library
| Need | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Limited dexterity | magnetic / velcro / large-button fasteners; front-open over pullover |
| Seated / wheelchair | shorter back-rise, no bulky back pockets, drape that sits well seated, jackets that don't bunch |
| Low vision | high-contrast pairings the user can identify by feel + Chitti's spoken label; consistent storage so favourites are findable |
| Senior comfort | breathable fabric, easy waistbands, weather-safe layers, non-slip footwear |
| Sensory sensitivity | tagless, soft seams, natural fibres |

## "Describe my outfit" (blind users — PRD F2/F0)
Chitti speaks: *"You are wearing a blue cotton kurta, black leggings, and brown
sandals. Suitable for office. Want me to suggest a dupatta from your wardrobe?"*
Always factual, warm, and ends with a useful next step.

## Output contract
- Teach the **why** (e.g. "front-open shirts are easier seated and look just as sharp").
- Offer the own-wardrobe option first.
- Never frame adaptive clothing as lesser. See [../accessibility/](../accessibility/) for per-archetype reviews.
