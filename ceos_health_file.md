## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Health File is India's voice-first family health record manager. Upload any medical document — blood report, prescription, MRI scan, discharge summary, insurance policy — Chitti reads it with DeepSeek vision, extracts key findings, translates medical jargon into plain language, sets follow-up reminders, and lets you ask "Wife ki diabetes ka history dikhao" in Hindi. AES-256-GCM encrypted. Never to server without explicit consent.

**Core Insight:** Indian families collect paper prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries — and lose them at the worst moment. A digital health file that speaks your language, answers questions about your own health history, and prepares you for the next doctor visit is the missing link.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the family health secretary — who stores every report, reads every result in plain language, reminds about follow-ups, and prepares you for every doctor visit with your complete history."
