# SOP — Free legal aid & entitlements (the moat)

Before any paid referral, run the value ladder ([../CONSTITUTION.md](../CONSTITUTION.md)):

1. **Educate** — explain the right/rule.
2. **Check free-aid eligibility** (`legalAid`) — s.12 LSA Act categories (woman, child,
   SC/ST, disabled, senior, custody, disaster, industrial, low-income).
3. **Route to the free channel** — NALSA 15100 / DLSA / Lok Adalat, and the topic helpline
   (`govtLegalLayer`): women 181, senior 14567, cyber 1930, child 1098, consumer 1915.
4. **Self-serve** — the engine answers (rights/deadline/checklist/scam) for ₹0.
5. **Low-cost option** — budget lawyer only for the gap.
6. **Paid expert** — only for genuinely HIGH-risk matters.

Rules: every eligible user must be told they are owed FREE aid; never let a "facilitator"
charge for what is free; final eligibility is the DLSA's call (state income limits vary).
Engine: `legalAid`, `govtLegalLayer`.
