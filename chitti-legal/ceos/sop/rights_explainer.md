# SOP — Explain rights (educate before escalate)

1. **Identify user type** & situation (police/employee/tenant/women/senior/consumer/cyber/student).
2. **Map rights** from the deterministic KB (`rightsCoach`) — current codes (BNS/BNSS/BSA 2023).
3. **Explain the legal basis** in plain language (Education > Fear).
4. **Give first steps** — the 3 concrete things to do now.
5. **Surface free help** — the right helpline + NALSA 15100 (Free Legal Aid > Paid Referral).
6. **Flag HIGH-risk** (arrest, court, serious notice) → recommend a lawyer.
7. **Offer to remember** the matter in the Legal Twin.

Rules: Explain rights **before** actions; never guarantee; cite the basis; route abuse to
protection (DV 181, cyber 1930, police 112). Engine: `rightsCoach`, `caseCompanion`.
