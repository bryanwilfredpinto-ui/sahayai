# QUALITY — Chitti Legal OS quality operating contract (LQOS)

> Legal is HIGH-risk. The quality bar is the merge-blocker; below it is a defect, not a
> feature gap. Mirrors the platform Quality v2 ([SAHAYAI_MASTER §6](../../SAHAYAI_MASTER.md))
> applied to law.

## Five quality layers

| Layer | Gate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Legal accuracy** | explanation ≥95% · rights ≥95% | a wrong right erodes trust |
| **2. Deadline safety** | limitation/jurisdiction = **100% deterministic, 0 critical errors** | a missed/wrong deadline can lose a valid case — P0 |
| **3. Anti-hallucination** | < 1% · engine never invents a section/citation/date | a fabricated section is worse than "I don't know" |
| **4. Accessibility** | = 100% (axe 0 serious/critical + four-user journeys) | the four users are the floor |
| **5. Safety / guardrails** | never lawyer/guarantee/predict/illegal; refuse + route abuse | law touches liberty & safety |

## P0 incidents (stop-the-line)

- A guaranteed outcome or "you will win" ([guardrails/no_guarantee.md](guardrails/no_guarantee.md)).
- A wrong deadline/limitation/jurisdiction shown as certain.
- A fabricated section/citation ([guardrails/hallucination.md](guardrails/hallucination.md)).
- Advising an illegal act or evasion of lawful process.
- A feature that a blind/deaf/mute/illiterate user cannot complete.

## How quality is enforced

- **Deterministic core** — the engine computes deadlines/jurisdiction/rights from a
  versioned table; the LLM only explains. No rupee… no *date* is LLM-generated.
- **Test-gated build** — every BO has a test; a failed test blocks the next BO.
- **Per-response widget** (🔊/🤖/👍/👎) on every result box, into the Founder dashboard.
- **Swarm HIGH-risk gate** — Legal skill changes require Sire's review before landing.
- **Server-enforced disclaimer** on every LLM response (never client-controlled).
