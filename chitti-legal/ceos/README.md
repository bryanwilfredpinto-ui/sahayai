# Chitti Legal OS — CEOS v1.0

**India's Legal Operating System for every citizen, business and legal professional.**
Not a legal chatbot, a contract generator or a lawyer directory — a Legal Education,
Rights-Navigation, Prevention, Documentation, Compliance, Memory and Accessibility
system in **one trusted dost**, in 26 languages, by voice, for free.

> Built 2026-06-07 to the **chitti-fashion / CA-OS CEOS bar**. Doctrine: **rules are the
> product, the LLM is an enhancement.** Every right, deadline, jurisdiction, helpline and
> red-flag is computed by a deterministic engine — it works with the internet down and
> DeepSeek 429. Chitti never files, signs, appears or guarantees. **Not legal advice.**
