# PERSONAS — who Chitti Legal OS serves

> The four-user accessibility floor (Blind · Deaf · Mute · Illiterate) is the design
> baseline; every persona below benefits, but the design call always goes to the most
> vulnerable user in the room.

## Citizen personas

| Persona | Core needs | Chitti modules |
|---|---|---|
| **Farmer** | Land records, crop-insurance claims, compensation, property disputes | Rights · Deadlines · Free legal aid · Doc checklist |
| **Student** | Education rights, scholarships, ragging/cyber-bullying, hostel disputes | Rights (student) · Scam shield · Free legal aid |
| **Employee** | Salary disputes, notice period, harassment (POSH), labour law | Rights (employee) · Deadlines (wages/POSH) · Doc checklist |
| **Homemaker / Woman** | Family law, domestic violence, property & inheritance rights | Rights (women) · Free legal aid (woman = always eligible) · Govt help (181) |
| **Senior Citizen** | Pension, property transfer, fraud protection, maintenance rights | Rights (senior) · Maintenance Tribunal · Scam shield · Elderline 14567 |
| **Tenant** | Eviction, deposit, repairs, rent increases | Rights (tenant) · Notice decoder (eviction) · Deadlines |
| **Consumer** | Refunds, insurance claims, builder delays, defective products, online scams | Consumer router · Doc checklist · Scam shield · NCH 1915 |

## Business personas

Shop Owner · MSME · Startup Founder · Manufacturer · Exporter.
**Needs:** registrations, licences, GST/IT notices, contracts, labour compliance,
recovery of dues (cheque bounce), deadline tracking.
**Chitti modules:** Notice decoder · Contract risk · Deadlines (cheque/GST) · Doc checklist.

## Professional personas

CA · HR · Doctor · Teacher · Lawyer. They use Chitti as a fast first-pass: decode a
notice, check a limitation period, map a right, or hand a client a plain-language
explanation — Chitti speeds them up, it does not replace their judgment.

## Accessibility personas (the floor)

| User | Primary mode | What Chitti guarantees |
|---|---|---|
| **Blind** | Voice-first | Every result read aloud; auto-read on first result; no visual-only error |
| **Deaf** | Visual + text + ISL | Every result is text + symbol + word (never audio-only); ISL panel |
| **Mute** | Tap-first | Every action reachable by tap; chips/buttons/selects for all input; ≥44px |
| **Illiterate** | Voice + icon-first | Icon-led chips/tabs; 🔊 on every result; no required reading |
| **Low-vision / Elderly** | Large text, simple language, slow speech | ≥16px base, AA contrast, reduced-motion/contrast/forced-colors, Senior mode |
| **Rural / Low-bandwidth** | Offline-first | Deterministic engine works with the internet down and DeepSeek 429 |
