Disclaimer — Chitti 2-Wheeler

Chitti 2-Wheeler is not a mechanic. Every answer is a guide. For any major problem (engine seize, brake failure, electrical fire, accident), see a trained mechanic in person.

Emergency protocol — family cascade only

If you say "accident ho gaya" or trigger SOS, Chitti rings your family through the Vaani cascade — confirm-with-Master → alarm → spouse → family → Chitti-to-Chitti relay. Chitti never auto-dials 100 / 108 / 112. You can override and dial yourself — but the cascade is the default.

Price quotes are bands, not finals

Anti-overcharge guard returns a fair-price band, not a single number. Always confirm with the mechanic on the floor.

OBD2 mode honesty

If the Bluetooth adapter is not paired, Chitti will not invent sensor readings — it will say so. Honest stubs over fake demos is a platform rule.

Camera scans are user-owned

Every photo (spare part, document, fake-product check) belongs to you. Never sold, anonymised before aggregate analysis, "Chitti forget" deletes everything.

Breakdown Coach Live P0
Step-by-step Hinglish when bike refuses to start.
  1. Safety first. Hazard lights ON. Side mein bike lagao. Helmet pehna rakho — traffic visibility ke liye.
  2. Fuel check. Reserve switch ON karo (agar hai).
  3. Check engine light. ON hai? Pair karo OBD2 adapter (Coming Soon) ya screenshot bhej do mechanic ko.
  4. Battery. Horn bajti hai? Indicators light hain? Nahi → battery low.
  5. Side-stand safety switch. BS-VI Activa / Splendor — agar side stand down hai, start nahi hogi.
  6. 5 minute aaram. Phir kick / self try karo.
  7. Agar start ho gayi — seedha mechanic ke paas jao. 40 km/h se upar mat jao. Hazards on.
  8. Agar nahi → SOS tab open karo. Family cascade fire hogi. RSA numbers neeche.
