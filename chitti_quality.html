Global standards followed

What we adopt — and what we deliberately refuse — is recorded in full at GLOBAL_BEST_PRACTICES.md. Headlines:

🇨🇳 China — GB/T 37668-2019

Accessibility design for elderly users. One-tap elder / braille mode, 18pt single-column layout, large touch targets. Wired in chitti_a11y.js.

🇨🇳 China — Voice-first finance UX

Speak-the-name flow inspired by Alipay / WeChat Pay elder mode. Used in Chitti Vaani, MedUPI, Sales.

🇦🇪 Dubai — UAE Charter for AI Development & Use (2024)

Five principles — inclusivity, transparency, accountability, safety, human oversight. Each mapped to a quadrail or hook in lib/quadrails.py + lib/hooks.py.

🇦🇪 Dubai — Happiness Meter

Thumbs-up / down on every response card. Stored via lib/feedback.py. Aggregated weekly.

🇦🇪 Dubai / TAMM — Multilingual minimum

UAE mandates 8 languages on government services. Our equivalent: minimum 4 Indian languages live before a Chitti is "shipped". 26-language registry in chitti_a11y.js.

🇸🇬 Singapore — Model AI Governance Framework v2

Risk-tier per Chitti: high (medupi, legal, ca, vaani), medium (shares, government), low (news, sales). Each tier has matching observability + evaluator coverage.

🇸🇬 Singapore — AI Verify principles

Eight principles — transparency, explainability, repeatability, safety, security, robustness, fairness, accountability. Mapped to evaluator passes in lib/evaluators.py.

🇸🇬 Singapore — SG Enable Inclusive Design Mark

Co-design with vulnerable users. Our four-user contract (blind, deaf, mute, illiterate) is the local equivalent. Visible in every CONTEXT.md.

🇸🇬 Singapore — WCAG 2.1 AA (continuous audit)

Manual via the BRAILLE.md checklist; axe-core CI planned. Spot-check on highest-traffic page of each Chitti weekly.

🇮🇳 India — DPDP Act 2023

On-device audit log on Chitti Vaani Android; never auto-uploaded. No third-party analytics. X-User-Token is a per-device UUID, not derived from user data.

What we refuse

  • Super-app monoculture (China WeChat / Alipay). Each Chitti is independently installable, deletable, auditable.
  • Mandatory national-ID linking (UAE Pass). Aadhaar is opt-in everywhere.
  • Centralised digital identity (Singpass). No Chitti-pass; no mandatory biometrics.
  • Social-credit feedback aggregation. Happiness meter is anonymised and per-product.

Chitti Quality's ongoing responsibility

  1. Monitors new global standards (China, Dubai, Singapore, EU, India DPDP) monthly; quarterly OWASP AI Top 10 review.
  2. Proposes improvements as GitHub issues with implementation plan + affected Chittis + effort/priority.
  3. Implements across all Chittis after pilot passes a full CHECKLIST.md for seven consecutive days.
  4. Reports weekly digest (Monday 07:00 IST) + monthly certification report to the Founder.
  5. Never lets standards slip — auto-rollback wired into lib/hooks.py for critical failures; immediate alert to the Founder.
