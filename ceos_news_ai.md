## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary

Chitti News AI is India's voice-first Professional Career Coach, AI Learning Companion, and AI News Platform — three things in one, accessed via one page, routed by Vaani based on user intent.

**Primary Purpose — Professional Career Coach:**
Chitti learns a course. Then Chitti teaches YOU — in your language, using examples from YOUR profession and life. A TA professional says "Main career mein aage badhna chahta hoon" — Chitti maps his career path, finds SHRM-CP and People Analytics certifications, reads the course itself, and teaches him daily in Hindi using recruitment analogies. He gets certified. His career grows. A farmer wants to learn AI — Chitti teaches Agentic AI using crop rotation and mandi pricing as analogies. The farmer gets certified without leaving his village.

**Secondary Purpose — AI Tool Discovery & News:**
Latest AI tools, research papers, and platform updates — curated for Indian professionals, summarised in Hindi, voice-read for accessibility.

**Third Purpose — Chitti Swarm Learning:**
User gives a big learning goal. Multiple AI agents each learn one part — courses, concepts, top practitioners, real-world applications — then combine into one unified roadmap and surface connections the user would miss alone.

**Core Principle:** Every concept taught using the user's own profession as the analogy. Never Silicon Valley examples for Indian farmers, drivers, or homemakers.

### 1.2 Vision
*"Be the personal career coach every Indian deserves — who maps your path, learns the course for you, teaches in your language using your life as the textbook, and celebrates every certification — so a farmer earns an AI certificate and a TA professional becomes a TA Head."*
