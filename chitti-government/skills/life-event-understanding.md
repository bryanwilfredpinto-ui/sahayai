# SKILL 4 — Life-Event Understanding

Map a life event → an ordered bundle of {documents, schemes, registrations, deadlines}.
Events: birth · daughter born · marriage · death · job loss · start business · buy
land/house · turn 18 · turn 60 · disability onset · move state · retirement. Ordered by
deadline urgency; each action tied to the affected family member. Example — "daughter
born": birth certificate (≤21 days) → Aadhaar → Sukanya Samriddhi → immunisation →
PMMVY → state girl-child scheme → school pathway. Backed by
