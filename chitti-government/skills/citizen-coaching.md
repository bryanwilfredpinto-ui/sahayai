# SKILL 8 — Citizen Coaching (Government Copilot)

Turn data into a plain-language, prioritised **action plan**. Answers *"What should I do
next?"* by merging eligibility + document gaps + deadlines + life events through the
Citizen Digital Twin, and computes the **Citizen Readiness Score** (Documents % ·
Schemes Claimed % · Benefits Missed · Readiness %). Teaches, doesn't just verdict —
every item carries What · Why · Documents · How · Deadline · Source. Voice-first for
blind/illiterate users. This is also the Vaani routing target
([SAHAYAI_MASTER §2](../../SAHAYAI_MASTER.md)).
