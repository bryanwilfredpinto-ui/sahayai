# SKILL 5 — Family Governance

Manage the whole household — parents, spouse, children — together: each member's
documents, schemes claimed, renewals due, readiness score. Extends the
[Citizen Digital Twin](../memory/citizen_digital_twin.md) `family[]` array.
**Anticipatory:** "Father turns 60 in 2 months → old-age pension + senior Ayushman 70+
at 70." On-device only ([guardrails/privacy.md](../guardrails/privacy.md)).
