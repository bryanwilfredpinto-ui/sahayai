# PRODUCT_VISION — Chitti Government (CEOS v1.0)

> Answers to [CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md) and [ROLE.md](ROLE.md).

## The one sentence

> Every citizen should be able to ask **"What am I eligible for?"** — by voice, in
> their own language, with no broker — and receive a **complete, honest, sourced**
> answer plus the exact next action.

## The shift — directory → operating system

| Yesterday (scheme directory) | Chitti Government (CEOS) |
|---|---|
| Citizen searches a list of schemes | Chitti tells the citizen what *they* qualify for |
| Static eligibility text | Deterministic eligibility engine over a Citizen Digital Twin |
| "Apply on the portal" | Document checklist + form pre-fill + deadline + nearest office |
| One-off lookup | Family Governance OS that tracks every member's documents + renewals |
| Reactive | **Anticipatory** — "your daughter turned 18, here are 4 schemes + voter ID" |
| Literate, English, urban | Voice-first, 26 languages, blind/illiterate/rural-first |
| Vulnerable to scam SMS | Built-in Government Fraud Shield |

## The seven world-class moats (the CHITTI MOAT)

1. **Life-Event Engine** — citizen says *"my daughter was born"* and Chitti knows
   every document, registration, scheme and deadline that event triggers.
2. **Family Governance OS** — the whole family (parents, spouse, children) managed
   together: their documents, schemes, renewals, in one place.
3. **Citizen Readiness Score** — a single number ("you are 72% ready; ₹X/6 benefits
   missed") that turns a vague anxiety into a concrete to-do list.
4. **Government Fraud Shield** — scans SMS / WhatsApp / email for scheme-scam
   patterns and explains *why* it's a scam, with the official reporting channel.
5. **Citizen Digital Twin** — Chitti remembers everything (on-device) so the citizen
   never re-enters their profile, and eligibility is computed instantly.
6. **Universal Scanner** — one camera button identifies Aadhaar / land record /
   pension letter / GST notice / scholarship form and routes it automatically.
7. **Government Copilot** — instead of searching portals, the citizen asks *"What
   should I do next?"* and Chitti answers with a prioritised action plan.

## North star

The day a **broker in a Tier-3 town loses a customer** because that customer's
illiterate mother used Chitti by voice to claim her widow pension herself — that is
success. Every design decision is measured against making the broker unnecessary.

## What we explicitly do NOT do

- We do **not** submit applications or sign on the citizen's behalf.
- We do **not** charge for anything that is free from the government.
- We do **not** store the Citizen Digital Twin on a server.
- We do **not** guarantee approval or guess eligibility.
- We do **not** auto-dial police; fraud routes to family cascade + 1930 /
  cybercrime.gov.in.
- We do **not** replace district-officer verification — we prepare the citizen for it.
