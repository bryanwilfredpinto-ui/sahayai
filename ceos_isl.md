## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti ISL is India's first AI-powered Indian Sign Language companion. It is NOT ASL. It is not a translation of foreign sign languages. It is ISL — the language of 6 crore deaf Indians — with its own grammar, its own vocabulary, and its own cultural context. Chitti ISL shows ISL animations alongside every Chitti response, teaches ISL to hearing people who want to communicate with deaf family members, and gives deaf Indians full access to SahayAI's 15 specialist Chittis.

**Core Insight:** India has 6 crore deaf and hard-of-hearing citizens. Not one mainstream app has first-class ISL support. WhatsApp doesn't. Google doesn't. Every government portal is inaccessible. Chitti ISL makes SahayAI the first platform in India where a deaf person can access government schemes, tax advice, legal help, medical information, and mental health support — all in their language.

### 1.2 Vision
*"Make SahayAI fully accessible to every deaf Indian — in Indian Sign Language, not ASL, not gestures — real ISL, animated alongside every response, so no deaf Indian is left behind by technology."*
