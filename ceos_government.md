## SECTION 1: PREAMBLE & VISION

### 1.1 Executive Summary
Chitti Government is India's voice-first guide to 30+ government schemes — PM Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, PMAY, PMBJP, PM Mudra, Sukanya Samridhi, and more. A user says "Mujhe PM Kisan milta hai?" — Chitti checks eligibility, shows documents needed, finds the nearest application center, and files a CPGRAMS complaint if they've been denied wrongly.

**Core Insight:** India has 300+ government schemes worth lakhs of crores. Most eligible citizens don't know they qualify. Chitti Government closes that gap — in 26 languages, by voice, for illiterate and rural users.

### 1.2 Vision
"Be the trusted sarkari sahayak — who knows every scheme, checks your eligibility in seconds, tells you exactly which documents to bring, finds the nearest office, and fights for your rights if denied."

|---|---|
| SOP 01 Scheme Find | Voice/text → classify scheme category → eligibility check → show matching schemes → document checklist → nearest office |
| SOP 02 Eligibility | User profile (farmer/worker/woman etc.) → cross-check vs scheme criteria → "Aap eligible hain" or "Aap eligible nahi hain — kyunki..." |
| SOP 03 CPGRAMS | Describe complaint → classify department → CPGRAMS portal link → step-by-step guide → 1800-111-555 |
| SOP 04 PIB Alert | Push notification on new scheme → "Nai yojana: [name] — kya aap eligible hain?" → eligibility check |
