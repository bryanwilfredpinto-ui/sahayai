- NEVER books service appointments on user's behalf
- NEVER dispatches mechanics
- NEVER certifies roadworthy / fitness
- NEVER performs OBD2 capture itself (queued under Chitti Mechanic)
- Service interval tables updated per manufacturer revision (annual)
- Spare-part prices: monthly diff per zone
- Recall notices: weekly diff against manufacturer + ARAI feeds
