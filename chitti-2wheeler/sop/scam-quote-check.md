# SOP — Scam Quote Check (Scam Shield playbook)

**Trigger:** *"mechanic ne ₹1 800 maanga oil change ke liye — theek hai?"*

## Steps
1. **Get the item + bike** — what work, which bike (the band depends on both). Ask if
   unclear; never assume an expensive item.
2. **Pull the fair band** — [Cost Agent](../swarm/cost-agent.md) → parts-only +
   parts+labour band ([MECHANIC_KNOWLEDGE §5](../skills/MECHANIC_KNOWLEDGE.md)) + city delta.
3. **Place the quote** —
   | Position | Symbol + word |
   |---|---|
   | Inside band | ✅ "theek hai — fair range mein" |
   | A bit above | ⚠️ "thoda upar — pooch sakte ho" |
   | Well above | 🚩 "fair band se kaafi upar lag raha" |
4. **Empower, don't accuse** — *"Splendor oil change ka fair range ₹350–500 hai; ₹1 800
   us se kaafi upar. Pooch sakte ho — genuine oil hai? Labour kitna?"* **Never** "yeh
   mechanic loot raha hai" or name-and-accuse ([../guardrails/scam-shield-rules.md](../guardrails/scam-shield-rules.md)).
5. **Question the need too** — if the diagnosis behind the quote is uncertain, flag
   that the **work itself** may not be needed: *"pehle confirm karo yeh kaam zaroori hai."*
6. **Per-response widget** — `tw_scam_shield`.

## Hard rules
- Bands, never one "correct" price ([../guardrails/never-claim-certainty.md](../guardrails/never-claim-certainty.md)).
- Never defame a named mechanic — judge the **quote vs band**, not the person.
- Honesty score is community-aggregate + anonymised, never a public accusation.

## Accessibility
Band + verdict spoken (blind), symbol+word (deaf, never colour-only), tap to ask
"what should I say to the mechanic?" (mute/illiterate get a spoken script).
