# SOP-003 — Overheating (STOP → coolant → leaks → fan → thermostat)

**COSDF L7 SOP-003.** Trigger: *"bike garam ho rahi hai"* / *"temperature light jal
gayi"* / *"engine se garmi / steam aa rahi hai"* / *"power kam ho gaya, knocking"*.
Overheating destroys an engine fast — the first action is **STOP**, not diagnose.
Air-cooled commuters and liquid-cooled bikes diverge after Step 0.

> **Hard floor:** a red temperature lamp or visible steam = 🔴 **STOP NOW.** Pull over
> safely, engine off, let it cool. **NEVER open a hot radiator cap** — scalding coolant
> sprays out ([GUARDRAILS P0](../GUARDRAILS.md)).

## Step 0 — STOP first (before any check)
1. **Pull over safely**, hazards on, engine **off**. Riding an overheating engine seizes it.
2. Steam, hissing, a burning smell, or knocking under load → 🔴 do not ride further.
3. Rider stranded / unsafe spot → [family-cascade SOS](./emergency.md) (confirm → alarm
   → family). **NEVER auto-dial cops.**
4. **Let it cool 15–20 min** before touching anything. The radiator cap stays shut while hot.

## Step 1 — Which cooling system? (the tree splits)
| System | Bikes | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| **Liquid-cooled** | KTM Duke/RC, RE Himalayan, Pulsar RS200, R15, Apache RTR, Dominar | coolant level + leaks + fan + thermostat → Steps 2–5 |
| **Oil-cooled** | Pulsar 150/180, some Apache | oil **level + grade**, blocked oil cooler fins, lean mixture |
| **Air-cooled** | Splendor, Activa, CB Shine, Platina | oil level/quality, blocked cooling fins (mud/grime), lean carb/FI, low-grade fuel knock, prolonged idling in heat/traffic |

For **air/oil-cooled**, the big three are **low/old oil**, **clogged fins**, and a
**lean/knocking mixture** — there is no coolant to check.

## Step 2 — Coolant level + condition (liquid-cooled, COLD ONLY)
- **Only when fully cold**, check the reservoir/overflow bottle between MIN–MAX (never
  the radiator cap while warm).
- Low → top up with the correct coolant (50:50 long-life, not plain tap water as a
  permanent fix). Topping up is 🟢/🟡; **finding why it's low** is the real job → Step 3.
- Coolant **brown/rusty or oily** → possible head-gasket / internal leak (🟠/🔴) — see
  [smoke_color SOP](./smoke_color.md) (thick sweet white smoke = coolant burning).

## Step 3 — Leaks (where is it going?)
| Sign | Likely source | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Puddle/drip under engine, sweet smell | radiator, hose, or water-pump seal | 🟠 |
| Wet, crusty residue on a hose/clamp | split hose or loose clamp | 🟡/🟠 (clamp = assisted) |
| Coolant disappearing with **no puddle** + white sweet exhaust smoke | **head gasket** (internal) | 🔴 Professional — see [smoke_color](./smoke_color.md) |
| Steam from the radiator front | radiator core leak / overheating boil-over | 🟠 |

## Step 4 — Cooling fan (liquid-cooled)
- Engine hot at idle/in traffic but fine while moving → the **radiator fan isn't
  cutting in**. Check: does the fan spin when the engine reaches temperature?
- Fan dead → fuse, fan motor, or temperature sensor/switch (🟠). Until fixed, **don't
  idle in traffic** — moving airflow masks it but the fault stands.

## Step 5 — Thermostat / pump / airflow
- Warms up oddly fast then overheats, or never reaches temp → **thermostat** stuck (🟠).
- Overheats at all speeds + coolant fine + fan fine → **water pump** weak / impeller (🟠).
- **Air/oil-cooled:** clogged cooling fins (clean the mud/grime — 🟡), wrong/old **engine
  oil** (change to spec grade — 🟢/🟡), lean mixture or knock on low-octane fuel (🟠).

## Step 6 — Verdict (box-element output)
Standard result card (🔊 / 🤖 / 👍👎 / ✏️🎙️ / 🌐), `data-chitti-response="tw_overheating"`:
- **Spoken-first safety call** — 🔴 STOP / let-cool sits at the top.
- Weighted cause + confidence band; cheapest sufficient fix first (often low oil /
  low coolant / clogged fins, not a head gasket — [Trust Agent](../swarm/trust-agent.md)
  guards against jumping to the expensive cause).
- Cost band + DIY tier; quote-check via [Scam Shield](./scam-quote-check.md).
- Cross-link: if the temperature **lamp** triggered this, see the
  [dashboard-warning-light SOP](./dashboard-warning-light.md).

## Hard rules
- **NEVER open a hot radiator cap** — wait until cold ([GUARDRAILS P0](../GUARDRAILS.md)).
- **Never coach riding an overheating engine** "just to reach home" — that seizes it. Stop.
- Plain water is a roadside get-home stopgap only, never the documented fix — correct
  coolant required.
- Don't jump to "head gasket gone" without coolant-loss-without-puddle + sweet white
  smoke evidence — over-diagnosis is a [Trust Agent](../swarm/trust-agent.md) red line.
- Emergency = [family cascade](./emergency.md), never cops.

## Accessibility
"STOP — engine band karo" is **spoken first and loud** (blind), shown as a flashing red
STOP symbol + word (deaf), with a big tap-to-confirm "I've stopped" button (mute). The
cool-down timer counts down audibly and visually. Photo of the leak / reservoir lets a
mute rider diagnose without speaking. Works on 2G, one step at a time.
