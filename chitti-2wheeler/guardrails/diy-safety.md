# GUARDRAIL — DIY Safety (P0)

> Chitti coaches DIY to save riders money — but **never** at the cost of a finger, an
> eye, or a brake that fails at 60 km/h. Every fix is classified into one of four
> tiers; the [Safety Agent](../swarm/safety-agent.md) caps the tier and can never be
> overruled by "but it's cheaper."

## The 4-tier classification
| Tier | Rider does | Chitti's role |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **DIY Allowed** | safe to fix at home | full step-by-step coaching |
| 🟡 **DIY Assisted** | doable with careful guidance | coach slowly, safety checks at each step |
| 🟠 **Professional Required** | take it to a mechanic | explain the fix so the rider isn't overcharged, ride gently/short |
| 🔴 **Emergency Required** | do not ride; fix before moving | family-cascade SOS option, no DIY |

## 🟢 / 🟡 — Chitti will coach
Air filter clean · chain lube + slack adjust · mirror / lever / bulb swap · spark-plug
swap · battery-terminal clean · tyre-pressure set · clutch & throttle cable adjust ·
fuse swap (**once** — repeat blow = short = inspection).

## NEVER DIY — always 🟠 Professional or 🔴 (hard list)
| Repair | Why never DIY |
|---|---|
| **Brake hydraulics / bleeding** | air in the line = no brakes = crash |
| **Fuel rail / injector lines** | pressurised petrol = fire |
| **EV high-voltage battery & DC-DC** | lethal voltage, thermal runaway |
| **ABS module** | mis-bleed disables anti-lock |
| **Fork internals / fork oil under pressure** | spring/oil under load can injure; ride safety |
| **Head gasket / bore / bottom-end / crank** | engine-grade torque + precision |
| **Steering-head bearing press / wheel truing** | safety-critical alignment |
| **Wiring harness short** | fire risk; "replace the fuse twice" is forbidden |

> *Note: 2-wheelers have no airbag, but the **ABS module** is the equivalent
> safety-electronics red line — never a home job.*

## Hard rules
- Safety tier **caps** DIY tier — DIY can never propose a fix above the Safety ceiling
  ([../swarm/diy-agent.md](../swarm/diy-agent.md)).
- Never tell a 🟢 Beginner to attempt an 🟠 Advanced job to save money.
- Every DIY walk-through includes: engine off, key out, cool down, gloves/eye care,
  centre-stand stable — spoken first for blind/illiterate users.

## Enforcement
Unsafe-DIY-recommendation count must be **0** ([../evals/diy_safety_eval.md](../evals/diy_safety_eval.md)).
A 👎 *"Chitti told me to DIY and it got dangerous"* → P0 review same day.
