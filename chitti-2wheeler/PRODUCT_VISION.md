# PRODUCT_VISION — Chitti Bike Doctor

## Mission (the one sentence)

> **"My bike has a problem — do I really need a mechanic, or can I fix it myself,
> and is this quote fair?"**

Chitti Bike Doctor answers that question, by voice, for every 2-wheeler owner in
Bharat — before they push the bike to a workshop, before they hand over cash.

NOT: *"Book a service."* NOT: *"Sell a spare part."* NOT: *"Drive engagement."*

## The problem we are solving

India has **~22 crore two-wheelers** — the Activa, the Splendor, the Pulsar are
the legs of the working class. Yet the rider is the most information-poor person
in the transaction:

- **Overcharged.** "Oil change ka ₹1 800" when the fair band is ₹350–500. The
  rider has no way to check.
- **Scammed.** A genuine ₹400 brake-shoe job sold as a ₹3 000 "complete brake
  overhaul." Fake bearings and fake brake shoes that *kill*.
- **Unaware.** Doesn't know the bike needs chain lube every 500 km, doesn't know
  the PUC expired (₹10 000 fine), doesn't know a tappet rattle is cheap now and
  catastrophic later.
- **Unable to explain.** "Awaaz aa rahi hai" — but which awaaz? The rider cannot
  describe a symptom in the mechanic's language, so the mechanic decides the price.
- **Forced to a workshop** for problems they could fix in five minutes with a
  ₹40 spark plug and a voice telling them how.

Chitti inverts this. Its first move is never "go to a workshop." It is:
**"Bataao kya ho raha hai — main dekhta hoon."** ("Tell me what's happening — let
me look.")

## Two modes — works for everyone, advanced for some

### Mode 1 — No External Device (the 90%)
The vast majority of Indian bikes have **no OBD2 port** and the rider has **no
adapter**. Mode 1 needs nothing but the rider's phone:

- **Voice** — describe the symptom in Hinglish / any of 26 languages.
- **Video / photo** — show the leaking gasket, the worn chain, the smoke colour.
- **Dashboard photo** — snap the warning lights; Chitti reads them.
- **Sound** — record a 10-second clip of the engine; Chitti ranks candidate faults.

Every Splendor, Activa, Jupiter and Passion owner is served on day one.

### Mode 2 — OBD2 / Bluetooth ELM327 (the advanced)
For bikes with a diagnostic port (RE 350+, KTM 250/390, Pulsar NS200+, R15v3+,
Dominar, most ABS bikes 2018+), the rider can pair a ₹400 ELM327 adapter over
**Web-Bluetooth**. Chitti reads live sensors (RPM, coolant temp, battery voltage,
O2, throttle), translates DTC codes into Hinglish, and runs **predictive**
maintenance (oil quality %, battery voltage-drop trend, misfire detection).

Mode 2 is a power-up, **never a gate**. The product is complete without it.

## The moat — what no app has built

1. **Accessibility-first, voice-first** — a blind rider says "Chitti, mera
   dashboard padho" and hears every warning light. No fashion-app-for-mechanics
   does this.
2. **DIY coaching** — not "go to a mechanic" but "yahan, ye karo" with tools,
   time, difficulty and a video — when and only when it is *safe*.
3. **Scam / overcharge detection** — upload the quote or invoice; Chitti returns
   the fair band and a Fair / High verdict by city.
4. **Vehicle memory twin** — Chitti remembers your model, service history, tyre
   age, battery age, brake age, and *predicts* what fails next.
5. **Preventive maintenance** — weather-aware, odometer-aware reminders before the
   part fails, not after.
6. **Sound diagnosis** — diagnose the awaaz the rider cannot describe.
7. **Swarm diagnosis** — 8 specialist agents vote; the rider sees a calibrated
   *"Battery 85% / Starter 10% / Fuel 5%"*, never a false certainty.
8. **Cost prediction** — every repair carries a fair-price band and the DIY saving.

## What Chitti Bike Doctor IS

- A **digital mechanic companion** that translates the bike's symptoms into the
  rider's language and the mechanic's quote into a fair-price verdict.
- A **teacher** — it explains *why* the fault happened, *how* severe it is,
  *whether* you can ride, and *what* it should cost.
- A **swarm of eight specialists** that vote before any diagnosis is shown.
- A **voice-first** companion usable end-to-end with zero reading.
- A **vehicle memory** that knows your specific bike and predicts its next failure.

## What Chitti Bike Doctor is NOT

- **Not a workshop booking funnel.** It never takes a cut of a service. Its success
  is measured in money the rider *kept*, not bookings it generated.
- **Not a parts shop.** It holds no inventory, sells no spare, earns no margin on a
  recommended part.
- **Not a certainty machine.** It never says "this is definitely the problem." It
  speaks in Likely / Possible and High / Medium / Low confidence, always.
- **Not a mechanic replacement.** For Professional-Required and Emergency jobs it
  routes the rider to a human — and arms them with the fair price first.
