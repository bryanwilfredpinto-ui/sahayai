# VISION — Chitti Technical (L1)

> Companion to [PRODUCT_VISION.md](PRODUCT_VISION.md). Mission + Vision in one page.

## Mission
Give every Indian retail trader — including the blind, deaf, mute, and illiterate — an honest,
risk-first, multi-timeframe technical-analysis companion that **speaks**, works in their language, and
never pretends to be certain.

## Vision
The first fully accessible technical-analysis OS: every chart pattern, indicator, signal, backtest,
and alert reachable by voice and icon, in 9+ Indian languages, on a ₹5,000 phone on 3G — with the
stop-loss and the honest confidence shown before the upside.

## What "world-class" means here
- **Risk-first:** no signal without a stop; position size from a risk budget.
- **Deterministic + auditable:** the same inputs always produce the same signal; every signal explains *why*.
- **Accessible by default:** four-user contract is a gate on every Build Order, not a final polish.
- **Honest:** confidence is measured (calibration), limitations are documented, NOT SEBI REGISTERED is permanent.

## Non-goals (v1)
Order placement / live trading · options & futures · crypto · paid tiers. Education + decision support only.
