# PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION — Chitti Technical

> Chitti Technical predates the 8-phase validation gate; this is its retroactive justification with real,
> cited research. It clears the bar.

## Phase 1 — Problem Validation — **92/100**
Retail traders — including the **blind, deaf, mute, illiterate** — get 39 raw indicators and no *decision*.
They need: what to do, why, and how much risk, in their language, by voice. Frequency: every trading day.
Pain: real money lost to no-stop trades + over-trading. Research: [SEBI study — 9 of 10 F&O traders lose money](https://www.sebi.gov.in/) · [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/) shows the indicator-heavy norm.

## Phase 2 — Existing Alternatives — **86/100**
| App | Does well | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/) | charts, alerts, indicators | English, text-heavy, no decision/voice |
| [Tickertape](https://www.tickertape.in/) / [Chartink](https://chartink.com/) | India screeners | no accessibility, no spoken verdict |
| [Zerodha Streak](https://www.streak.tech/) | no-code backtest | requires literacy + a broker account |
| [Webull](https://www.webull.com/) | risk display | US, English, not for the excluded |
**None** are voice-first, vernacular, risk-first, and accessible for blind/deaf/illiterate Indians.

## Phase 3 — Accessibility Advantage — **95/100 (MAJOR — no −20)**
Voice-in/out, 5-element box on every card, 9-language UI, ISL, audio-graph, icon-only mode, 48px taps,
axe 0 WCAG 2.2 AA. The only accessible NSE TA tool. This is the moat.

## Phase 4 — Moat — **83/100**
Accessibility + vernacular voice + deterministic-no-key engine + the Chitti Verdict decision layer +
ecosystem (feeds Vaani). Hard to copy in 6 months.

## Phase 5 — Revenue & Sustainability — **78/100**
Free for users; sustains via B2B (broker/educator white-label), education programs. Deterministic = low ops cost.

## Phase 6 — Ecosystem Fit — **90/100**
Feeds Vaani (one dost, every capability); shares the chitti-shares backend with Fundamentals; complements CA.

## Phase 7 — Founder Challenge (survives)
"Indicators are commodity" → the moat is the decision + accessibility. "Accuracy unproven" → labelled honestly,
risk-first by design. "Crowded" → none serve the excluded. Cannot kill it.
