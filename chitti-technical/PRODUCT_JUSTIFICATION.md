# PRODUCT_JUSTIFICATION — Chitti Technical (the technical-call engine)

> I am Founder · CTO · Architect · VC · Market Analyst · Devil's Advocate · Accessibility Expert.
> My job is to STOP bad products. Scope validated: a **pure technical-analysis call engine** — BUY/SELL/WAIT
> + Entry/Stop/Target across user-chosen timeframes, chart+39-indicator verification, call journal, backtest.
> **No fundamentals. No order placement. Educational, NOT SEBI.**

## Phase 1 — Problem Validation — **90/100**
- **Exact problem:** retail traders get either (a) raw charts/indicators they can't interpret, or (b)
  "100%-accurate" signal-sellers who lie and lose them money. The **blind, illiterate, non-English** are
  locked out of technical analysis entirely.
- **Pain:** severe — money lost to no-stop trades + false signals. [SEBI: ~9 of 10 F&O traders lose money](https://www.sebi.gov.in/).
- **Frequency:** every trading day. **Who:** retail traders incl. blind/deaf/illiterate/rural/non-English.
- **Company-sized?** Yes — crores of retail traders + a national financial-inclusion mandate.

## Phase 2 — Existing Alternatives — **86/100**
| Competitor | Does well | Does badly / gap |
|---|---|---|

**Why switch:** nobody gives an **honest technical DECISION** — trend/entry/stop/target — to a non-reader,
non-English, or blind user, **free, with the risk shown before the reward.** Even the most accessible app
(Robinhood) explicitly has **no technical analysis** for the blind.

## Phase 3 — Accessibility Advantage — **95/100 (MAJOR — no −20)**
The research proves the void: accessible apps don't do TA; TA apps aren't accessible; blind traders use a
spreadsheet. Chitti delivers the **full call by voice + 9 languages + ISL + haptic + sonification**, decision-first.
