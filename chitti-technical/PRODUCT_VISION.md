# PRODUCT_VISION — Chitti Technical

## Mission

Help **every NSE trader** understand the six things that actually decide a trade:

- **Trend** — which way is the higher timeframe pointing?
- **Momentum** — is the move strong or tiring?
- **Risk** — what do I lose if I am wrong?
- **Entry** — where is a sensible price to get in?
- **Exit** — where do I take profit?
- **Stop Loss** — where am I proven wrong?

…**without** requiring years of technical-analysis experience.

## Vision

> A farmer in Bihar.
> A student in Mumbai.
> A retired teacher in Chennai.
> A blind trader.
> A deaf trader.
> An illiterate trader.
>
> **All should receive the same institutional-grade technical analysis** — in
> their own language, by voice if they cannot read, with the risk shown before
> the reward.

Technical analysis today is gated behind English jargon, expensive terminals,
and YouTube gurus selling certainty. Chitti Technical removes all three gates:

- **No jargon** — Chitti Explain turns `RSI = 31` into *"momentum is oversold;
  this does not mean buy; wait for trend confirmation."*
- **No paywall** — free, voice-first, on a ₹5,000 phone over 2G.
- **No false certainty** — every signal shows its confidence, its stop loss, and
  the exact condition that would prove it wrong.

## The promise

Chitti Technical never tells you to buy.
It teaches you to **read the chart, size the risk, and decide for yourself** —
and it does that for the trader who cannot see the chart, cannot hear the audio,
cannot type the query, or cannot read the screen.

## What "world's most trusted" means here

| Pillar | What we do | What we refuse |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust** | Show confidence + invalidation on every signal | Guaranteed-profit / multibagger language (banned, see [guardrails/](guardrails/)) |
| **Accuracy** | Multi-timeframe confluence, 43+ indicators, Roshan composite | Single-indicator "buy because RSI < 30" calls |
| **Explainability** | Plain-language *why* on every number | A bare verdict with no reasoning |
| **Risk** | Entry zone · stop loss · 3 targets · RR · position size on every BUY/SELL | A target with no stop loss |
| **Accessibility** | Voice-first, ISL, full-language UI, 5-element box | Colour-only signals; English-only screens |
| **Transparency** | "NOT SEBI REGISTERED" never hidden; data freshness shown | Pretending to be registered advice |

## Non-goals (what Chitti Technical will never become)

- A broker — it never places, holds or routes an order.
- A tipster — it never sends "today's sure-shot."
- A real-time terminal — quotes refresh **on manual request only** (Sire's
  decision), keeping cost, battery and 2G-data honest.
- A registered investment adviser — it is **educational analysis only**.
