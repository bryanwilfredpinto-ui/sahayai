## LIVE — works today (engine)

- **38 technical indicators** — RSI, MACD, EMA/SMA stack, Stochastic, Williams %R,
  CCI, ROC, ADX, Aroon, Supertrend, Parabolic SAR, Ichimoku, Bollinger, ATR,
  Keltner, Donchian, OBV, Force Index, CMF, MFI, VWAP, Elder Ray/Impulse, TTM
  Squeeze, Awesome Oscillator, Vortex, Chandelier Exit, Hull MA, and more.
- **Roshan Indicator ⭐** — Sire's custom composite (RSI vs its own 20-SMA),
  favourited by default.
- **NSE stock / index / ETF analysis** via the existing `chitti-shares-api` engine.

## PLANNED — building now (CEOS rebuild)

- **BUY / SELL / HOLD scanner** with confidence band and confluence score.
- **Multi-timeframe analysis** — Long-term (Monthly→Weekly→Daily) · Positional
  (Weekly→Daily) · Swing (Daily→4H) · Intraday (4H→1H); higher timeframe governs.
- **Entry engine** — entry zone + ideal / aggressive / conservative entries.
- **Stop-loss engine** — price · % · ATR · support-based; no signal without a stop.
- **Target engine** — Target 1/2/3 with RR ratio.
- **Chitti Explain** — every number in plain language, in your language; never says "buy".
- **Configurable charts** — RSI / Williams %R / Stochastic etc. as a separate pane
  or overlaid on the candles, your choice, remembered.
- **Screener** — full NSE universe by market-cap tier (Nifty50 / Large / Mid /
  Small / Micro) + indicator filters + Roshan filter, manual "Run screen".
- **Portfolio Mode** — your private trade ledger (open / closed / PnL / risk).
- **10-agent swarm** — Trend · Momentum · Volume · Pattern · Roshan · Risk ·
  Confluence · Trust · Accessibility · Explain.
- **Audio Trade Summary** — Trend→Entry→Stop→Target→Confidence spoken for blind users.
