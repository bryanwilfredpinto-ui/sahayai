# ACCESSIBILITY — Illiterate trader (P7)

> A trader who cannot read must run the **full** BUY/SELL/STOP/TARGET flow with
> **zero reading**, by icons + voice, in their language, on 2G.

## Requirements (all mandatory)
- **Icons carry every action** — 📈 BUY · 🛑 STOP · 🎯 TARGET · 🔊 EXPLAIN ·
  🔄 REFRESH · ⭐ FAVOURITE — paired with voice labels.
- **Voice-everything** — every number and verdict is spoken in the user's language.
- **Picture/logo menus** — stocks shown as recognisable tiles, not text lists.
- **Voice confirmation** — "Say HAAN to set this alert" (Golden Rule), tap-safe too.
- **2G-ready** — small payloads, deferred images, manual refresh (already the model).
- **No reading required** end-to-end — the product is usable by listening + tapping.

## The signal, with zero reading
> 🔊 "RELIANCE. Medium buy. Buy near twenty-eight sixty. Stop at twenty-eight
> twenty — there you are wrong. Target twenty-nine hundred. You risk forty to make
> eighty. This is teaching, not advice."

…with 📈 / 🛑 / 🎯 icons drawn on the chart at those prices.

## Language
- Selecting Bangla/Telugu/Tamil/etc. re-renders the **whole** screen and switches
  voice-out — the illiterate user never meets an English word they must decode
  (except the indicator proper-nouns, which are spoken letter-by-letter).

## Anti-patterns (defects)
- A flow that requires reading a label to proceed. ❌
- Text-only stock lists with no logos/voice. ❌
- A number shown but not speakable. ❌
